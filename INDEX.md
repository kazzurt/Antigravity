## How this collection is organized

- Files are grouped into **numbered topic folders** (`01_…`–`07_…`), plus `_archive/` (originals).
- `00_Knowledgebase/` is the project control layer: mission, assumption ladder, capability model, current best path, UAP cases, candidate mechanisms, ranked threads, constraints, structured source digests, evidence register, and relationship map.
- **Most binary sources have a plain-text twin.** For each established `Name.pdf` / `Name.docx` there is normally a co-located **`Name.md`** containing auto-extracted text with a provenance header. The Ning Li primary set is represented by a structured source digest and deep-dive report rather than full text twins. The original binary always remains authoritative for equations, figures, and exact layout.
- The **synthesis reports** in `01_` are already native Markdown — read these first; they cite and summarize much of the primary material in the other folders.
- **Naming convention:** `Author-Title-Year`, hyphenated, ASCII-only. No spaces, accents, or symbols.
