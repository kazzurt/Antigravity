## Executive judgment

The project does not need a fifth candidate material attached to the same passive-shield idea. It needs architectures that repair the exact missing physical functions exposed by the completed work:

1. **more than one physical propagation channel**, so an unavoidable massless or harmonic mode is not mistaken for a screened one;
2. **a real covariant reference sector**, rather than asking an ordinary condensate amplitude to generate a graviton mass by analogy;
3. **an active conversion process**, so energy and momentum exchange appear in the equations rather than after the fact;
4. **a source with the required anisotropic stress**, rather than a large conventional mass current of the wrong tensor form;
5. **an external geometric reservoir**, so a low-proper-acceleration trajectory change is not silently treated as reactionless.

Five ideas survive this requirements-first generation pass. The leading proposal is a **phase-programmed bimetric mode converter**, internally called a *metric-polariton transformer*. It would not annihilate Earth's field. A material-controlled shell would rotate the combination of two spin-2 fields seen by matter, redirecting part of an externally sourced metric perturbation into another propagation eigenmode. Earth or a remote emitter supplies the momentum reaction. This is the most concrete new implementation of the project's externally supported worldtube architecture.

The best immediate work package is therefore a **Tensor-Mode Transformer Gate 0**, combining Ideas 1--3: solve a static two-metric slab, determine whether its massless constraint mode can actually be converted, supply the missing reference fields with a relativistic supersolid EFT, and then calculate the sidebands of a periodically driven boundary. This should be a theory calculation before an apparatus program.

Two high-risk wildcards should remain outside that critical path: a relativistic domain-wall/double-layer source matched directly to the inverse-designed shell stress, and conventional-GR curvature swimming as a conservation-complete geometric-phase benchmark.

## Why the problem must be decomposed differently

The knowledgebase has already fixed several numbers and theorems that should now guide creativity rather than merely constrain it:

- A useful `1 g` gradient over `10 m` corresponds to the small dimensionless metric change

  `gL/c^2 = 1.09 x 10^-15`.

  The difficulty is not producing a large dimensionless metric amplitude. It is terminating and supporting the gradient at a finite boundary.

- In the simplest flat-spatial-slice worldtube, a `0.10 m` transition layer requires anisotropic stress of order

  `p_req = c^2 g/(8 pi G d) = 5.25 x 10^27 Pa`,

  or integrated stress `Sigma_req = 5.25 x 10^26 J m^-2`.

- A single superconducting current produces a rank-one London--GEM response: one screened combination and one harmonic combination. A penetration depth is therefore not proof that all physical gravitational modes are expelled.

- A scalar-controlled metric mass has a formal `99%` attenuation scale of only

  `m_eff c^2 >= hbar c ln(100)/d = 9.09 micro-eV`

  for `d = 0.10 m`. Its failure was not this wavelength. It was the absent reference/Stueckelberg sector, portal normalization, finite-shell solution, and whole-system reaction channel.

- A compact, conserved, universally coupled body follows the background geodesic at monopole order. Internal rearrangement alone does not provide a commanded center-of-mass force. Any successful architecture must show an external stress connection, radiated momentum, altered charge, exotic monopole/stress, or global boundary reservoir.

These results suggest a higher-level design rule:

> Do not ask whether a material "repels gravity." Ask whether a covariant field theory contains the necessary modes, whether a controllable phase changes their mixing or boundary conditions, whether the resulting stress matches the worldtube tensor, and which external system receives the opposite four-momentum.

## Idea 1 -- Phase-programmed bimetric metric-polariton transformer

### Central idea

Replace passive shielding with **mode conversion**. Introduce two dynamical metrics, `g_mn` and `f_mn`, whose perturbations mix into one massless and one massive spin-2 eigenmode. Ordinary matter couples universally to one ghost-safe physical metric. A shell order parameter `chi` changes the local mass/mixing matrix, causing the matter-coupled perturbation to rotate between propagation eigenstates across the wall.

The optical analogy is not a gravity claim; it is a mathematical design pattern. In a polariton or neutrino system, the production/detection basis need not equal the propagation basis. Ghost-free bigravity already has this feature for gravitational waves. The unexplored project question is whether a **bounded, material-localized mixing profile can act on the stationary gravitoelectric sector**, not merely on propagating waves.

A schematic covariant starting point is

```text
S = (M_g^2/2) integral sqrt(-g) R[g]
  + (M_f^2/2) integral sqrt(-f) R[f]
  - m^2 M_eff^2 integral sqrt(-g) V(sqrt(g^-1 f); chi)
  + S_chi[g, f, chi]
  + S_m[g_phys(g, f, chi), psi].
```

The interaction potential must retain the constraint structure that removes the Boulware--Deser mode. `g_phys` must be unique for hull, occupants, payload, clocks, and light; otherwise the architecture is a composition-dependent fifth-force sail, not a protected physical worldtube.

### Proposed causal chain

`Earth or remote source -> physical-metric perturbation -> phase-programmed mixing shell -> redistribution between massless and massive tensor modes -> modified physical metric in the occupied region -> universal craft response -> stress/recoil returned to the external source and shell supports`

For terrestrial hover, Earth is the reaction partner. For deep space, the incident field must come from a remote emitter or the craft must radiate a mode carrying opposite momentum. The idea receives no credit for self-contained thrust.

### Quantitative foothold

For a `0.10 m` wall, suppressing an evanescent massive eigenmode by a factor of 100 requires `m_eff c^2 >= 9.09 micro-eV`. That number is not itself forbidding. The decisive issue is whether the physical static field can be rotated into that eigenmode while the universal massless constraint mode is kept from simply crossing the wall.

Write the linear slab problem as

```text
[partial_z^2 I - M^2(chi(z))] H(z) = J(z),
H = (h_g, h_f)^T.
```

Diagonalizing `M^2` gives a local mixing angle `theta(z)`. The project must solve the complete constraint system and junction conditions, not just this transverse wave analogue. The key observable is the transfer matrix from an exterior source perturbation to the physical lapse gradient inside and to the force on the shell.

### Why this could be a real advance

Bigravity already predicts a difference between production/detection and propagation bases and therefore gravitational-wave oscillations. What is new here is the attempt to make that mixing **localized, phase-controlled, static-field aware, and tied to a complete source-recoil ledger**. A targeted search located the established ingredients but no primary paper demonstrating such a condensed-matter-localized gravitoelectric transformer. That is a search-scoped novelty statement, not proof of priority.

### Strongest objection

Diffeomorphism invariance leaves a massless spin-2 mode. If universally coupled matter necessarily sources that mode with nonzero overlap everywhere, the static Earth field will retain an unscreened component. Composite matter couplings can also reintroduce a ghost above some scale or violate equivalence-principle and cosmological bounds. A mixing angle drawn on paper is not yet a legal material interface.

### Next action and stop condition

Produce a planar and spherical **Bimetric Transformer Gate-0 calculation**:

1. choose a ghost-free bimetric action and one conservative matter coupling;
2. derive scalar, vector, and tensor constraints across a smooth `chi(z)` interface;
3. solve the `omega = 0` source problem before studying waves;
4. calculate interior lapse, tides, shell stress, exterior multipoles, and source recoil;
5. map PPN, inverse-square, gravitational-wave, and strong-coupling limits onto the same parameters.

Stop if the massless static overlap has a nonzero lower bound incompatible with useful cancellation, or if the required material-dependent matter coupling loses universality/constraint closure. Continue only if a finite transfer matrix shows controllable physical-metric redistribution and a force on the external source/shell pair.

## Idea 2 -- A relativistic supersolid as the missing Stueckelberg medium

### Central idea

The failed metric-Higgs proposal asked a scalar material order parameter to do too much. A massive or modified metric generally requires reference fields that define temporal and spatial structure. Relativistic media EFT supplies exactly the right mathematical object: four derivatively coupled scalars `Phi^A`, whose background values act as the comoving coordinates of a continuous medium. With internal spatial symmetry, the general phase is a relativistic supersolid; in unitary gauge, modified or massive gravity can emerge.

This suggests a precise replacement for the vague portal `lambda |Psi|^2 phi^2`:

```text
C^AB = g^mn partial_m Phi^A partial_n Phi^B,

S_ref = integral sqrt(-g) [Lambda_ref^4 P(C^AB)
        + M_Pl^2 m^2(chi) U(g^-1 f(Phi))].
```

One phase-like field can supply a clock/order coordinate and three phonon/displacement fields can supply spatial coordinates. The claim is **not** that a laboratory supersolid already gives gravitons mass. The claim is that this is the first material language in the project with the correct field count and symmetry structure to ask the question coherently.

### Proposed causal chain

`electromagnetic/strain drive -> ordered reference-field phase Phi^A -> phase-dependent tensor mass or mixing in a shell -> interaction with an Earth/remote metric field -> universal physical-metric response -> recoil through the external field and the driven medium`

The material is a gate and reference structure, not the primary energy source.

### Why this repairs an exact failure

The earlier scalar mass wall had a nonempty attenuation wavelength but no origin for the Stueckelberg/reference sector. Relativistic self-gravitating-media theory supplies four fields and a symmetry classification. It also forces the project to distinguish:

- ordinary laboratory supersolid phonons and phase modes;
- a dark or fundamental relativistic reference medium;
- an analog metric seen only by quasiparticles;
- the physical metric seen universally by matter.

Only the last two-field coupling problem is relevant to propulsion. An emergent acoustic metric alone remains an analog and earns no gravity claim.

### Strongest objection

The natural energy scale of a condensed-matter order parameter is microscopic, whereas a tensor mass term is normalized by `M_Pl^2 m^2`. The previous Gate-0 estimate put the evaluated mass-potential backreaction near `10^16 J m^-3`. Unless a symmetry, sequestering mechanism, or pre-existing fundamental medium provides this stiffness, inserting the coefficient by hand merely renames the missing new physics. A real crystal also breaks Lorentz symmetry and couples composition-dependently; preferred-frame and radiative-stability bounds are severe.

### Next action and stop condition

Perform a **Stueckelberg material dictionary** calculation:

1. enumerate the invariant operators of a relativistic supersolid with `Phi^0` and `Phi^i`;
2. identify which laboratory order parameters could realize only the symmetry/gating role;
3. derive the helicity spectrum, Hamiltonian constraints, sound speeds, and ghost/gradient conditions in ordered and disordered phases;
4. calculate the technically natural size of `m^2(chi)` after loops of the material fields;
5. test whether a smooth finite interface preserves the constraint count;
6. derive the largest portal consistent with equivalence-principle, preferred-frame, inverse-square, and gravitational-wave limits.

Stop the laboratory-material implementation if the needed coefficient remains Planck-normalized without a protecting mechanism. In that case retain the formalism only as a theory of a fundamental background medium and do not call ordinary supersolidity a gravitational source.

## Idea 3 -- Floquet gravity converter: convert the zero mode instead of trying to expel it

### Central idea

Both the London--GEM and static mass-wall analyses ran into unscreened or constraint-like zero modes. A static boundary cannot do work on a static field. The creative alternative is an **actively modulated boundary** that mixes frequencies and momenta.

Let a controlled phase make a local coefficient periodic:

```text
m^2(t, x) = m_0^2(x) + delta_m^2(x) cos(Omega t + phi(x)).
```

A field component at frequency `omega` then couples to sidebands `omega + n Omega`. The pump supplies or absorbs `n hbar Omega`; propagating sidebands can carry energy and momentum. A spatial phase gradient `phi(x)` makes the modulation directional, like a traveling-wave mixer rather than a reciprocal shield.

Parametric resonance of propagating tensor modes is established in scalar--tensor EFT. The new question is harder and more useful: **can a diffeomorphism-consistent driven boundary convert any part of a stationary gravitoelectric constraint into propagating scalar/tensor sidebands, or do the Hamiltonian and momentum constraints force the pump's ordinary stress-energy to be the only source?**

### Proposed causal chain

`electrical RF/optical input -> traveling material phase modulation -> time-dependent metric/mediator mixing -> DC-to-sideband conversion of an externally sourced field -> altered interior transfer function and outgoing radiation -> force on craft/shell -> opposite momentum in Earth/source, pump supports, and radiation`

This chain makes the momentum destination observable. In terrestrial hover, the external Earth field and supports can carry the reaction. In autonomous deep space, purely radiative thrust obeys the lower power bound

`P >= F c = M_c a c`.

For `M_c = 10^5 kg` and `a = 1 g`, this is `2.94 x 10^14 W`, before inefficiency. Floquet conversion does not evade that bound; its value is diagnostic control, possible field redirection, and a sharply modulated laboratory signature.

### Laboratory foothold

A `1 kg` source at `0.10 m` produces `6.67 x 10^-9 m s^-2`. A differential detector sensitivity of `10^-12 m s^-2` would probe a phase-dependent conversion or transmission fraction near `1.5 x 10^-4`, provided source motion and all conventional sidebands are independently monitored.

The experiment would use a modulated source mass outside a phase-switchable shell, plus two inside detectors and one outside reference. The distinctive signal is not a DC weight change but sidebands at `Omega`, source frequency `omega_s`, and controlled combinations `omega_s +/- n Omega`, with phase reversal when the traveling modulation reverses.

### Strongest objection

The Newtonian lapse is a constrained field, not a freely propagating photon. A periodic coefficient may only add the pump's own ordinary stress-energy, leaving the source constraint unchanged. Even where tensor parametric resonance exists, gravitational coupling may be Planck-suppressed and useful conversion bandwidth may require an astrophysical path length.

### Next action and stop condition

Start with a scalar toy model but end with the full covariant constraints:

1. derive the Floquet scattering matrix and exact energy/momentum balance for a finite driven slab;
2. repeat for the scalar sector of the leading bimetric action;
3. identify whether the `omega = 0` source solution couples to radiative sidebands;
4. calculate the ordinary stress-energy sidebands of the actuator as the standard-physics null;
5. specify a dual-detector phase-reversal experiment only if a nonexcluded portal predicts a larger, differently scaled signal.

Stop if the only source term is the actuator's conventional energy/stress or if conversion efficiency is unavoidably Planck-suppressed below any credible test. Continue if the action predicts a gauge-invariant sideband in the physical metric with a distinct spatial transfer function.

## Idea 4 -- Relativistic defect-stress shell matched to the worldtube tensor

### Central idea

The project has mostly asked low-energy matter to create a large gravitational response. Reverse the logic: identify a relativistic field configuration whose **native stress tensor already has the tension and anisotropy** required by the worldtube boundary, then ask whether material phases can nucleate or steer it.

A scalar domain wall is the simplest candidate:

```text
S_sigma = integral sqrt(-g) [-1/2 (partial sigma)^2
          - lambda/4 (sigma^2 - v^2)^2]
```

Its wall carries surface energy and tangential tension rather than merely rest mass. Quadratic-curvature theories also admit gravitational double layers with dipole-like curvature distributions, external flux, and scalar pressure/tension required by conservation. These structures are qualitatively closer to the inverse-designed shell tensor than superconducting mass currents.

### Surprising scale result

The required integrated stress is

`Sigma_req = c^2 g/(8 pi G) = 5.25 x 10^26 J m^-2`.

Using `1 GeV^3 = 4.12 x 10^21 J m^-2`, a natural-unit wall tension `sigma ~ v^3` reaches this scale at

`v ~ 50 GeV`.

That is an electroweak-scale field amplitude, not the Planck scale. This does **not** make the shell practical. A 5 m radius wall with comparable positive surface energy stores

`E_wall ~ 4 pi R^2 Sigma_req = 1.65 x 10^29 J`,

equivalent to `1.84 x 10^12 kg`, and it generates a large exterior field. The useful insight is narrower: the source *tensor form* may be natural to a relativistic defect even while its total energy is catastrophic.

### Proposed causal chain

`high-energy reservoir -> nucleation/pinning of a scalar or curvature defect -> anisotropic wall stress and metric junction -> low-tide interior profile -> force on the complete shell/craft -> reaction in the external field, formation apparatus, and any emitted wall/field radiation`

A condensed-matter layer could at most pin or switch the defect; it cannot supply `10^29 J` by coherence.

### Possible escape to test, not assume

A paired wall, curvature double layer, or bimetric defect might cancel the exterior monopole while retaining the pressure anisotropy that terminates the interior gradient. In GR, this generally demands negative energy or comparably severe stresses. In `R + alpha R^2` gravity, distributional double layers are mathematically permitted, but some components still violate energy conditions and the higher-curvature scale is tightly constrained.

### Next action and stop condition

Run an **inverse junction-design study**:

1. solve Israel junction conditions for scalar domain walls against the least-cost worldtube metric;
2. repeat in one ghost-controlled quadratic or scalar-tensor completion that permits finite-thickness double layers;
3. compute total monopole mass, exterior multipoles, stability spectrum, formation path, and collapse/expansion times;
4. determine whether paired defects can reduce exterior mass without simply hiding equal positive and negative inventories;
5. calculate what material operator, if any, can pin the defect without supplying its energy.

Stop if exterior quietness and the required tension demand an unstable negative-energy component, a ghost below the wall scale, or stored energy still near the raw `10^29 J`. Continue only if the modified field equations produce a stable finite-thickness defect whose total source ledger is parametrically smaller than the GR shell bound.

## Idea 5 -- Curvature pump / spacetime swimmer as a conventional-GR wildcard

### Central idea

An extended body in curved spacetime can, in special circumstances, change its trajectory through cyclic internal deformations. The effect is described covariantly through multipole coupling to external curvature; it is not reactionless motion in flat space. The external gravitating body and curvature field are the reservoir.

This is not a hover solution and it will not cancel Earth's field. It is worth a bounded study because it is one of the few accepted routes by which an internally actuated, freely falling system can deviate from a reference geodesic while its center is not supported by ordinary thrust. It therefore provides a clean GR control case for the project's more speculative frame-engineering ideas.

### Scale estimate

For a small body of size `L`, a generous geometric displacement scale per optimized cycle is

`Delta x ~ C Riemann L^3`,

where `C` is a dimensionless shape-loop factor that is generally below unity and may vanish by symmetry. Near Earth's surface,

`Riemann ~ g/(c^2 R_E) = 1.71 x 10^-23 m^-2`.

At `L = 10 m`, even `C = 1` gives only

`Delta x ~ 1.7 x 10^-20 m per cycle`.

At an implausible `1 MHz`, this is only `1.7 x 10^-14 m s^-1`. The terrestrial craft window is therefore effectively closed by ordinary curvature-size scaling. The research opportunity is to ask a different question: whether a strong-curvature but low-tidal trajectory near a supermassive compact object, or a deliberately generated external field, admits a useful cumulative geometric phase.

### Proposed causal chain

`actuator energy -> cyclic internal quadrupole/stress loop -> multipole coupling to external Riemann curvature -> small worldline displacement/geometric phase -> opposite momentum/angular momentum in the external gravitating system and field`

### Why keep it

It supplies three valuable controls:

- a fully covariant example where internal state cycles and external geometry jointly matter;
- a rigorous ceiling on claims that cyclic piezoelectric, Mach-effect, or phase-transition devices "push on spacetime";
- a possible orbital station-keeping niche in strong curvature even if it is irrelevant to terrestrial `1 g` control.

### Next action and stop condition

Derive a **low-tide curvature-pump bound** using the Dixon multipole equations and the 2025 covariant swimming formalism:

1. maximize displacement per cycle subject to craft size, stress, internal velocity, and occupant-tide limits;
2. scan Schwarzschild and Kerr backgrounds, including supermassive objects where horizon curvature can be modest;
3. track energy and angular momentum transferred to the central body/field;
4. compare the result with photon, electric-sail, and ordinary gravity-assist performance.

Close this lane as propulsion if no astrophysically accessible region beats conventional station-keeping by many orders. Retain the equations as a calibration/null framework for internal-deformation experiments.

## Recommended next program: Tensor-Mode Transformer Gate 0

The next report should not separately defend five speculative mechanisms. It should answer one decisive composite question:

> Can a stable two-metric theory with a phase-localized relativistic reference medium convert a stationary, externally sourced physical-metric perturbation between eigenmodes, and can periodic control generate a gauge-invariant, momentum-accounted sideband large enough to test?

### Eight-week theory sprint

1. **Weeks 1--2 -- Static eigenmodes:** choose one ghost-free bimetric action, linearize about a terrestrial weak-field background, and solve a finite slab with a prescribed mixing profile. Preserve all lapse/shift constraints.
2. **Weeks 2--4 -- Finite boundary and conservation:** compute the physical-metric transfer function, shell stress, source recoil, exterior multipoles, and the force on a closed spherical shell.
3. **Weeks 3--5 -- Reference-sector completion:** replace the prescribed profile with four relativistic-medium fields and an order parameter. Count degrees of freedom and calculate stability/strong-coupling scales across the transition.
4. **Weeks 5--7 -- Floquet extension:** modulate the gate, derive its scattering matrix and sideband momentum flux, and separate new-field signals from actuator stress-energy.
5. **Week 8 -- Decision:** map current bounds, state the surviving parameter window or prove it empty, and either specify a phase-reversal transmission experiment or close material-localized tensor conversion.

### Gate criteria

**Go** only if all are true:

- one unique physical metric gives universal hull/occupant/payload response;
- the stationary physical lapse-gradient transfer can change by a nonzero, gauge-invariant amount;
- the massless mode, constraint algebra, and interface junction conditions are explicitly controlled;
- the order phase changes a derived coefficient rather than an unexplained gravitational permeability;
- source, shell, pump, field, and external reaction four-momenta close;
- a laboratory transfer or sideband signal occupies parameter space not already excluded.

**No-go** if any of the following is unavoidable:

- a universally coupled massless mode transmits the full static field;
- the material interface reintroduces a sixth ghostlike mode or strong coupling below `1/d`;
- the portal requires an unprotected Planck-normalized coefficient;
- the only modulated signal is conventional actuator stress-energy;
- a closed shell changes its interior field but cannot acquire support/recoil as a complete system.

If no-go, promote the relativistic defect-stress study long enough to determine whether modified junction physics can reduce the source tensor cost. If that also fails, the project should return to externally generated moving metric wells/field sails rather than passive shielding.

## What not to do next

- Do not begin with a new superconductor, magnetic texture, phonon, or DNP recipe before an action identifies the invariant operator and predicts the physical-metric transfer function.
- Do not treat a penetration depth, analog metric, negative effective mass, resonance, or large susceptibility as gravitational gain by itself.
- Do not build a generic "weight anomaly" experiment whose distance, phase, composition, and source scaling are not unique to a surviving action.
- Do not search arbitrary warp metrics without matching their Einstein tensor to a source sector, formation history, exterior field, and momentum ledger.
- Do not count interior weightlessness as whole-craft hover until the shell and source reaction are solved.
