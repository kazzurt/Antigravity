# Mission — Reverse-Engineering Frame Decoupling

## Project purpose

This project searches for the most viable physical and engineering route to a technology that can separate a craft's local inertial experience from its motion relative to the surrounding environment.

The working premise is that at least one engineered system of this kind exists and that some persistent UAP reports are imperfect observations of its consequences. The project does not require every UAP case to be extraordinary, nor does it assume that the system's origin, mechanism, or full performance envelope is already known.

## Problem anchor

> Reverse-engineer the minimum capability stack that would make the strongest reported UAP performance physically coherent, under the adopted premise that an engineered frame-decoupling system exists.

The core model is an **engineered local inertial frame**, or protected worldtube: the craft and occupants remain at low proper acceleration while the worldtube changes motion relative to distant observers and the surrounding medium.

## Research posture

The knowledgebase operates in two modes at once:

1. **Discovery mode:** assume the destination exists and search broadly for mechanisms, control variables, engineering handles, signatures, and useful analogs.
2. **Audit mode:** preserve source provenance, conventional explanations, null results, scaling gaps, no-go results, and missing measurements.

The destination is a project premise. Every proposed bridge remains contestable.

## Success criteria

The project succeeds by producing:

- a ranked capability-to-mechanism map;
- a small portfolio of research threads with explicit failure conditions;
- a list of decisive observations and proxy experiments;
- a durable record of what has been tried, constrained, or invalidated;
- a current best path that changes when evidence or theory warrants it.

It does not succeed merely by accumulating aligned claims, unusual images, patents, or equations with suggestive terminology.

## Non-goals

- Proving that every unresolved observation is an exotic craft.
- Inferring the origin or occupants of a technology from maneuvering alone.
- Treating mathematical analogy as physical equivalence.
- Treating FTL as an automatic consequence of inertial decoupling.
- Upgrading a source because it agrees with the project premise.
- Forcing every investigation into a single preselected mechanism.

## Primary research question

What controllable physical configuration could keep a bounded interior region near-geodesic and low-tidal while changing that region's external trajectory and environmental coupling?

## Supporting questions

- What carries or exchanges momentum and energy?
- What suppresses occupant acceleration and tidal stress?
- What boundary conditions control interaction with air, water, plasma, radiation, and solid matter?
- Which field invariants or constitutive properties would have to be engineered?
- What observable signatures would distinguish frame decoupling from ordinary propulsion or sensor effects?
- Which subscale experiments probe a necessary ingredient without claiming to demonstrate a complete drive?

## Operating rule

For every proposed mechanism, record both:

> **If the premise is true, how could this source help explain or control it?**

and:

> **What does this source independently establish, and what would falsify the proposed connection?**
