# Dunning-Davies and Dunning-Davies — Maxwell-like Gravitational Equations

## One-line thesis

The authors propose replacing partial time derivatives with convective derivatives in Maxwell-like gravity equations and present a translated flat-space gravitational-vortex model associated with Carstoiu.

## Core content

- Reviews Heaviside-style gravitational analogues of Maxwell's equations.
- Presents a second field, labeled the gravitational vortex `Omega`, coupled to the ordinary gravitational field.
- Replaces `partial/partial t` with `D/Dt = partial/partial t + v dot grad` to make the source-free equations Galilean invariant.
- Suggests similar reasoning changes how one interprets the need for special relativity.

## Why it matters here

The paper records a historical/nonstandard line of thought and exposes concrete assumptions about media, covariance, and propagation speed. Those assumptions can be turned into falsification questions.

## Evidence assessment

Nonstandard theoretical argument with no new experimental validation. Its Galilean-invariance premise conflicts with the Lorentz-covariant structure used by modern electromagnetism and GR, so it should be treated as a hypothesis to audit, not a replacement framework.
