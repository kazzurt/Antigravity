## Why this source cluster matters

This cluster tests whether the niobium London-moment residual could be related to Ning Li's surviving counterflow intuition and identifies physically real replacements for Li's unsupported coherent-ion derivation.

## Consolidated finding

The Tate measurement is a condensate–lattice electromechanical response measurement, not a direct measurement of isolated Cooper-pair rest mass. It is sensitive to missing entrainment, neutral modes, surface effects, and genuinely new rotation or gravity couplings.

Li's strongest intuition now has a better formulation:

> Seek a neutral eigenmode of a multicomponent coherent medium whose electric current is canceled or screened while its mass or angular-momentum current remains nonzero.

## Status of the three questions

### Tate–Li relationship

- **Plausible structural relationship:** both involve condensate motion relative to an ionic lattice.
- **No demonstrated identity:** Tate did not measure ionic flow or gravity.
- **Useful numerical clue:** the `92 ppm` residual could be represented by an ionic inertial fraction of only about `1.1 × 10^-9` of a niobium atom per Cooper pair. This is a sensitivity estimate, not a physical interpretation.
- **Later claim:** Hoang et al. report a smaller niobium residual near `18 ppm`, but the data package and apparatus documentation require independent audit.

### Gravitomagnetic permeability

- The electromagnetic analogy remains useful as a search heuristic.
- No recent literature establishes a large scalar material `μ_g` for superconductors.
- Modern work is better described through nonlocal stress-energy response kernels, TDGL dynamics, tensor gravitational-wave response, or generalized constitutive equations.
- An effective `μ_g(ω,k)` should be derived from a complete theory and a defined observable, not imported from Meissner attenuation.

### Collective-field candidates

1. **Oppositely charged multicomponent condensates:** strongest complete model; naturally supports neutral mass superflow.
2. **Axial/chiral phonons:** strongest real ionic angular-momentum carrier; direct mechanical evidence now exists.
3. **Carlson–Goldman modes:** established charge-neutral superfluid/normal counterflow near `T_c`.
4. **Superconductor–exciton hybrids:** charged supercurrent can theoretically convert to neutral coherent flow.
5. **Entrainment:** correct mathematical framework for dissipationless drag between coherent components.
6. **Leggett modes:** observed distinct neutral condensate coordinate, but no generic net mass current.
7. **Spin counterflow:** proves charge-canceling persistent non-charge current is possible in superconducting theory, but does not produce a mass current.

## Evidence posture

- Tate 1989/1990: high-quality primary measurement; unexplained residual under the stated theory and error model.
- Hoang 2020: peer-reviewed reported measurement; evidence package incomplete for an extraordinary claim.
- Multicomponent, Carlson–Goldman, Leggett, entrainment, and phonon-angular-momentum literature: strong evidence that the relevant kinds of collective variables exist in physics, but not that they coexist in niobium or generate anomalous gravity.
- Large superconductor gravitomagnetic permeability: unverified theoretical proposition.
