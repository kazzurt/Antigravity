# Okunev — The Unified Theory of Gravitation and Electromagnetism

## One-line thesis

The paper models radiation as diverging massless streams and gravity as converging motion, then claims a shared geometric mechanism for electromagnetism, gravitation, redshift, Mercury's orbit, and light-propagation observations.

## Core content

- Starts from continuous space, uniform time, and infinitesimal motion elements.
- Derives inverse-square gravitational behavior using geometric scaling arguments.
- Claims noncentral combined electromagnetic/gravitational forces explain Mercury's open orbit.
- Claims instantaneous source-response properties explain Michelson-type and binary-star observations.

## Why it matters here

It is a compact example of a kinematic unification attempt and provides several empirical claims that could be checked against precision tests.

## Evidence assessment

Only four pages, with no reliable venue/date metadata and no quantitative comparison to modern constraints. The instantaneous-response premise is especially consequential and unsupported in the document.
