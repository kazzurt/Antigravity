# Alzofon theory and dynamic-nuclear-orientation weight claims (1981–2026)

## One-line thesis

Frederick Alzofon proposed that gravity is a mutual contraction of overlapping particle fields and that ordering nuclear spins, then letting them relax, reduces weight; later authors (Ionescu, Hathaway, Falcon Space) reinterpret and test the idea, but no document supplies an equation for the effect.

## Core content

- **Formalism (2010):** random light-signal offsets are promoted to four extra coordinates, giving a flat interval with two time-like directions. Imposing a null condition yields a relation `p₀² − (E₀/c)² = (mc)²` read as the origin of inertial mass.
- **Gravity model:** a neighbouring particle raises a particle's mass, its Compton-scale size contracts by Le Chatelier's principle, and the neighbour is drawn in; the result is identified with Newtonian gravity by assumption.
- **Control claim:** nuclear spin order relapsing to disorder draws energy from the gravitational interaction (adiabatic-demagnetization analogy).
- **1994 experiment:** ~0.5 g aluminium with iron inclusions, 0.33 T, ~9.5 GHz, 6 ms pulses, claimed 1.1 mg change.

## Key claims audited

See the [derivation audit](../../01_Reports-and-Syntheses/Alzofon-Theory-Derivation-Audit-2026.md). In brief:

- The mass relation is an algebraic identity for any motion; the mass is an input.
- The gravity model has no source for `G m_p²/ħc = 5.9 × 10⁻³⁹`, a short-range mechanism, and the wrong sign of interaction energy under its natural reading.
- The only quantitative reading of the control claim gives `Δw/w ≤ 2.2 × 10⁻⁹` at complete polarization, against a claimed `2.2 × 10⁻³`.
- The 6 ms relaxation time is the standard Korringa value for aluminium at 300 K.
- Falcon's trace matches neither a polarization law (shape) nor a relaxation-rate law (size, by `10⁴`).

## Evidence assessment

**Rating `D` for the theory; `B` for Stark et al. as a controlled null; `C` for Prutchi as careful secondary analysis; commissioning-stage for the Falcon poster.**

Supported use: provenance of the DNO-weight hypothesis; the 1994 parameters; a worked example of microwave, thermal, and magnetic artifacts at the milligram scale.

Do not treat as established: any connection between nuclear spin order and weight; Alzofon's formalism as a derivation of mass or gravity; the Falcon feature as a weight change.

## Open item

The 1981 paper should be read for any equation relating orientation or relaxation to force. That is the only item that could change the verdict on the control claim.
