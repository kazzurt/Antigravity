## One-line thesis

General relativity can be modified in theoretically controlled ways either by relaxing full diffeomorphism/Lorentz invariance or by retaining diffeomorphism invariance and adding algebraically eliminable auxiliary fields, but extra degrees of freedom, stability, strong coupling, causal structure, and local matter-gradient tests sharply restrict the resulting phenomenology.

## What the thesis actually covers

The broad title can be misleading for this project. This is not a survey of antigravity or propulsion. It develops two specific modified-gravity programs around Lovelock's theorem and the Ostrogradsky instability problem.

### 1. Hořava gravity and broken Lorentz symmetry

- Hořava gravity uses a preferred spacetime foliation and anisotropic scaling to improve ultraviolet power counting, at the price of breaking full diffeomorphism and Lorentz invariance.
- Its infrared limit is related to Einstein-Æther theory when the æther is restricted to be hypersurface orthogonal at the action level.
- The thesis separates **projectability** from **detailed balance**. It argues that many scalar-mode pathologies come from projectability and presents a detailed-balance, nonprojectable version with well-behaved low-energy spin-0 and spin-2 dynamics.
- That result is a low-energy consistency result, not a proof of full quantum renormalizability, ultraviolet predictivity, or empirical correctness. The thesis itself leaves those issues open.

### 2. Rotating black holes and universal horizons

- The thesis derives exact rotating black-hole solutions in three-dimensional Hořava gravity, with anti-de Sitter solutions confined to the `η = 0` sector and additional flat/de Sitter cases.
- In Lorentz-violating gravity, the usual event/Killing horizon need not block arbitrarily fast gravity-sector modes. A **universal horizon** can act as the stronger causal boundary.
- Some of the derived black holes lack a universal horizon. This makes the definition and thermodynamics of a black hole model-dependent once arbitrarily fast propagation is allowed.
- These are `2+1`-dimensional exact solutions used to probe causal structure; they are not four-dimensional craft geometries or evidence for controllable horizons.

### 3. Gravity with non-dynamical auxiliary fields

- The thesis treats Eddington-inspired Born-Infeld gravity, Palatini `f(R)` gravity, and Brans-Dicke theory with `ω₀ = -3/2` as examples of theories in which additional fields can be eliminated algebraically.
- After elimination, Einstein's equations acquire a divergence-free correction built from the metric, the ordinary matter stress-energy tensor, and derivatives of that tensor. Metric derivatives remain second order, but higher derivatives of matter are generic.
- To next-to-leading derivative order, the broad class reduces to two independent parameters, `β₁` and `β₄`, aside from the cosmological constant. This provides a theory-independent constraint framework.
- Sharp density transitions make the metric overly sensitive to matter gradients. For the thesis's conservative example with a source radius of order meters and a transition length of order microns, the characteristic scale obeys `λ_β ≪ n^-1/2 mm` (where `n` is the derivative-expansion order). The thesis describes this as roughly 30 orders of magnitude below the Hubble scale.
- Modified Friedmann equations can contain `ρ²` corrections and, for some signs/equations of state, a cosmological bounce. The same local constraints make large-scale effects difficult without additional scales or fine tuning.

## Project relevance

This thesis is most valuable as a **Gate 0 checklist** for candidate gravity-control theories:

1. State exactly which symmetry is broken or which field is added.
2. Count the propagating degrees of freedom after constraints are imposed.
3. Check ghost, gradient, tachyonic, and strong-coupling behavior in the operating regime.
4. Specify how general relativity is recovered where it is already tested.
5. Treat matter interfaces and density gradients as possible constraint amplifiers, not automatically as useful actuators.
6. Audit every proposed causal boundary against all modes of the theory, not only metric-null signals.

For the engineered-worldtube program, the universal-horizon discussion is a useful warning about startup, communication, and shutdown in Lorentz-violating models. The auxiliary-field analysis is a warning that integrating out a gate field can move the new physics into higher derivatives of stress-energy and make material boundaries pathological or tightly constrained.

## Evidence assessment

**Rating: A for scholarly theoretical background; no direct actuator evidence.** The thesis clearly separates assumptions and contains original work tied to refereed papers listed in its collaboration statement. Its principal results are mathematical and model-dependent. It is a 2014 snapshot, not a current review of experimental bounds or the subsequent status of Hořava/Einstein-Æther and auxiliary-field gravity.

## What it does not establish

- controllable gravity, antigravity, inertial-mass reduction, or reactionless propulsion;
- a source sector capable of producing a craft-scale metric;
- energy-momentum closure for a vehicle or isolated laboratory device;
- a four-dimensional traversable or steerable universal horizon;
- that power-counting renormalizability proves quantum consistency;
- that a cosmological correction surviving the formal expansion also survives local and particle-physics constraints.
