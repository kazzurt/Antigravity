# Hively and Loebl — Classical and Extended Electrodynamics

## One-line thesis

The paper argues that classical electrodynamics is overdetermined and adds a scalar degree of freedom that permits longitudinal scalar waves and modified conservation laws.

## Core content

- Recasts Maxwell theory as seven independent scalar equations in six unknowns and calls the system overdetermined.
- Introduces an additional scalar `C`, with new terms in Ampere's and Gauss's laws.
- Predicts free-space longitudinal/scalar waves, revised energy-momentum balance, and propagation through conductive enclosures.
- Reports preliminary monopole-antenna and shielding tests that the authors interpret as favoring extended electrodynamics.

## Why it matters here

The paper offers explicit, testable predictions relevant to resonator and shielding experiments. The most valuable next step would be a preregistered independent replication with calibrated leakage controls and conventional coupling models.

## Evidence assessment

Published in *Physics Essays* (2019, DOI 10.4006/0836-1398-32.1.112), but the key experimental evidence is preliminary, partly funded by a related company, and linked to an author's patent. The paper itself acknowledges that replication is needed. Treat the effect claims as unconfirmed.
