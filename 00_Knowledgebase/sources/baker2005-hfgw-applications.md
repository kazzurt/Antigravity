# Baker — Applications of High-Frequency Gravitational Waves

## One-line thesis

Baker proposes HFGW communications, propulsion, imaging, navigation, astronomy, and nuclear/physics applications, but the paper supplies speculative application chains rather than a demonstrated generator, detector, or validated system budget.

## Core content

- Robert M. L. Baker Jr., AIP Conference Proceedings **746**, 1306-1314 (STAIF 2005), DOI `10.1063/1.1867259`.
- Proposed uses include through-Earth communications, HFGW propulsion, tomography, navigation, and focused high-flux interactions.
- The paper relies on hypothetical laboratory generators, Li-Baker-style detection, and speculative high-temperature-superconductor lenses or mirrors.
- No laboratory HFGW generation or detection result is presented.

## Relationship to JASON

This paper is a direct claims target of JASON JSR-08-506. JASON rejects the detector sensitivity by many orders of magnitude, finds artificial generation/detection infeasible, and rules out the proposed communications, imaging, and propulsion applications on the analyzed physics and engineering assumptions.

## Evidence assessment

**Rating: D — conference advocacy/claim inventory.** Retain it to identify proposed applications and their dependency chain. Do not cite it as evidence that HFGWs can be generated, focused by superconductors, detected in the laboratory, or used for propulsion.
