# Costa and Herdeiro — A Gravito-electromagnetic Analogy Based on Tidal Tensors

## One-line thesis

The physically clean analogy between gravity and electromagnetism is expressed through covariant tidal tensors, not by treating gravitational potentials and fields as literal copies of Maxwell theory.

## Core content

- Rewrites Maxwell-like relationships using electromagnetic and gravitational tidal tensors.
- Gives three realizations of the analogy, including linearized gravity, ultra-stationary spacetimes, and a derivation of the Papapetrou force on a gyroscope.
- Constructs gravitational and electromagnetic invariants analogous to `E^2 - B^2` and `E dot B`.
- Explains where common GEM analogies are valid and where spin-2 gravity prevents a literal correspondence.

## Why it matters here

This is the best conceptual guardrail in the batch. It supports using GEM as a controlled approximation while preventing unjustified extrapolation from electromagnetic hardware to gravitational effects.

## Evidence assessment

Rigorous scholarly derivation within standard relativity. It establishes mathematical correspondences, not propulsion or gravity control.
