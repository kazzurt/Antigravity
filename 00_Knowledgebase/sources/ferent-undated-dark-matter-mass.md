# Ferent — Dark Matter Gives Mass to the Elementary Particles

## One-line thesis

The author proposes that dark-matter-associated gravitons generate elementary-particle mass and introduces a new constant, superluminal gravitons, negative momentum, and a replacement quantum-gravity formalism.

- Rejects the Higgs mechanism, LIGO detections, relativity-based gravity, and several mainstream quantum-gravity programs.
- Introduces a “Ferent constant” near `1.59 x 10^-76 J s` and substitutes it into proposed momentum and uncertainty equations.
- Assigns gravitons superluminal speed, negative momentum, negative mass, and negative energy.
- Repeats personal discovery claims throughout a 227-page compilation.
