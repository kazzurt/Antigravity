# Inan — Superconductor Meissner Effects for Gravitoelectromagnetic Fields

## One-line thesis

Inan derives coupled electromagnetic and gravitomagnetic London equations in harmonic coordinates and correctly exposes the absence of an isolated gravito-Meissner effect, while the project's later boundary/eigenmode audit qualifies the paper's claim of generic joint magnetic and gravitomagnetic expulsion.

## Core content

- Nader A. Inan, *Frontiers in Physics* **10**, 823592 (2022), DOI `10.3389/fphy.2022.823592`; local source is arXiv:2201.12969 v1.
- A space-plus-time particle Lagrangian is used to derive canonical momentum and a mixed electromagnetic/gravitational minimal-coupling rule.
- For a neutral superfluid or a charged supercurrent exposed only to `B_G`, the gravito-Ampere sign produces oscillatory/paramagnetic behavior rather than exponential Meissner screening. The estimated niobium spatial period is `~3.3 × 10^15 m`, irrelevant on laboratory scales.
- Earth-potential and gravitomagnetic corrections to the ordinary London depth are negligible: the terms compared in the paper are roughly `10^18 m^-2` versus `10^-30 m^-2`.
- The paper then states that, when both magnetic and gravitomagnetic fields are present, the magnetic coupling converts the `B_G` equation to a decaying solution with approximately the London penetration depth.
- The paper explicitly notes that `B_G = curl(h)` is coordinate dependent and points to curvature-based treatments for invariant observables.

## Project audit

The 2026 London-GEM eigenmode calculation re-solved the coupled equations without discarding the homogeneous zero mode. The response matrix is rank one: generic boundary data do **not** make both fields decay. Joint decay requires the special boundary relation `B_G = rB`, with `r = μ_G m_e/(μ_0 e) ≈ 4.22 × 10^-32 C/kg`. This preserves Inan's important "no isolated gravito-Meissner" result while narrowing the claimed coupled expulsion.

## Evidence assessment

**Rating: B — modern standard-framework analysis, useful but boundary-condition-sensitive.** It is a strong constraint on literal gravity/Meissner analogies and a valuable derivational starting point. It is not evidence for gravitoelectric shielding, terrestrial levitation, or large material gravitational gain.
