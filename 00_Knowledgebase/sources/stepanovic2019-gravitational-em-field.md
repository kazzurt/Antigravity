# Stepanovic — The Gravitational and Electromagnetic Field

## One-line thesis

An “equivalent electromagnetic” field, introduced through the equivalence principle, is used to formulate an alternative description of static, stationary, and quasi-stationary gravity.

## Core content

- Imports electromagnetic field formalism into gravitational field equations via an equivalent potential `A_i = g_{0i}/√g_00` — structurally the standard stationary-field (Landau–Lifshitz-type) metric decomposition that also underlies GEM.
- Derives alternative metric and motion equations and compares selected predictions with GR.
- Limits the framework to static, stationary, and quasi-stationary fields; the author states the PE-based potential definition fails for general variable fields.
- First-order classic tests are degenerate with GR: light deflection comes out `4m/c²R` (eq. 3.45) and the spectral-line shift is standard.
- Claimed differences from Schwarzschild-type solutions enter at second order in the metric coefficients; the flagship discriminant is a Mercury perihelion advance `Δφ = 5πGM/[c²a(1−e²)]` (eq. 3.49) — exactly `5/6` of the GR coefficient, stated by the author as “about 37″ per century” and “available for measurement.”

## Why it matters here

This source is useful as a catalog of alternative equations and possible observational discriminants. Its restriction to near-stationary fields and lack of a validation program limit direct engineering use.

## Evidence assessment

Independent alternative framework with minimal external validation visible in the document. The conclusions are based on internal derivation, not discriminating experiments.

**2026-07-30 re-read — flagship discriminant already falsified.** The paper’s own decisive prediction, `≈37″/century` for Mercury versus GR’s `6πGM/[c²a(1−e²)]` ≈ `43.0″/century`, is contradicted by the measured anomalous advance `42.98 ± 0.04″/century`; MESSENGER-era ephemerides confirm the GR value at the `~10⁻⁵` fractional level. The required deviation (`≈6″/century`) is excluded far beyond measurement uncertainty, with no new experiment needed. Because the first-order sector is degenerate with GR and the second-order sector fails, the framework is excluded as a GR competitor within its own claimed quasi-stationary solar-system regime — the classic failure mode of EM-analog gravity substitutes (cf. Nordström-type scalar theories, which also fail on perihelion). Residual project value is the formalism catalog and claim provenance only.
