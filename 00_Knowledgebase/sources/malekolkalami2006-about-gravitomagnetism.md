# Malekolkalami and Farhoudi — About Gravitomagnetism

## One-line thesis

Simplified special-relativistic derivations of gravitomagnetism differ by a factor of two depending on whether gravitational time dilation is included; the authors argue the semi-relativistic result matches linearized GR.

## Core content

- Compares a special-relativity-only construction with one augmented by gravitational time dilation.
- Finds the inferred gravitomagnetic strength in the latter approach is twice the former.
- Uses linearized GR examples, including a moving infinite line source, to argue for a preferred potential normalization.

## Why it matters here

Useful for tracking normalization and factor-of-two traps when translating between GEM conventions. It should not be used as evidence that simple electromagnetic source scaling produces large gravity effects.

## Evidence assessment

Scholarly arXiv preprint with a narrow theoretical purpose. Its value is interpretive and conventional rather than experimental.
