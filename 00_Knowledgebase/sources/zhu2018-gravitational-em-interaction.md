# Zhu — Gravitational-Magnetic-Electric Field Interaction

## One-line thesis

Zhu infers large, measurable electromagnetic modification of gravity from gravitational redshift and energy-conservation arguments, but does not derive the proposed coupling from a covariant action or provide experimental validation.

## Core content

- Yin Zhu, *Results in Physics* **10**, 794-798 (2018), DOI `10.1016/j.rinp.2018.07.029`.
- The paper proposes `Δg = fB/sqrt(G μ_0)` and an analogous electric-field relation, with `f` taken from terrestrial gravitational redshift.
- It claims strong magnetic or electric fields could change local gravitational acceleration by measurable amounts and discusses propulsion applications.
- The introduction acknowledges that the prior capacitor and superconductor anomaly experiments it cites were not repeatable.

## Main problem

The argument treats gravitational redshift as photon energy transferred to a local gravitational-field energy reservoir and then inserts constants to obtain dimensions. It does not supply a stress-energy coupling, field equation, gauge-invariant observable, conservation-complete apparatus model, or controlled measurement. The predicted large response is also inconsistent with the absence of such signals in high-field laboratories unless an unmentioned selection rule suppresses them.

## Evidence assessment

**Rating: D — peer-reviewed venue, unsupported heterodox derivation.** Useful as a concrete claim and proposed scaling law to falsify; not a validated modification of GR or an engineering relation.
