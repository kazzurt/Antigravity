# Michelsen — Gravitomagnetism: The Myth and the Legend

## One-line thesis

This educational deck gives a compact weak-field GR introduction to gravitomagnetism, emphasizing its velocity-dependent, tensor-origin character, factor-of-two conventions, and observable gyroscope/orbital effects.

## Core content

- Eric L. Michelsen, originally 2005 and revised 2010; 18-slide teaching presentation.
- Derives the weak-field GEM analogy from a perturbed metric and shows why only selected metric components reduce to vector-like equations at the stated order.
- Covers gravitomagnetic forces, gyroscope precession, Gravity Probe B, and lunar-orbit perturbations.
- Repeatedly warns that sign and factor-of-two conventions vary and that the vector analogy is a weak-field reduction of a rank-2 theory.

## Limitations

The deck predates the final Gravity Probe B publication and later frame-dragging measurements. Some historical precision statements should therefore be checked against current primary results before citation. It is a teaching source, not a primary derivation or material-response analysis.

## Evidence assessment

**Rating: B — useful dated educational source.** Good for conceptual orientation and convention warnings; defer quantitative claims to modern primary sources and the collection's Mashhoon/Costa digests.
