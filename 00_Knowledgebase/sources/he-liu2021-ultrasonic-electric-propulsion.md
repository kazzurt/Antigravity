# He and Liu — Capillary Standing Waves in Ultrasonic Electric Propulsion

## One-line thesis

He and Liu experimentally and numerically study how ultrasonic frequency, amplitude, and liquid-film thickness control capillary standing waves used to emit charged propellant droplets in a conventional electric micropropulsion system.

## Core results

- Weiguo He and Xinyu Liu, *Acta Astronautica* **185**, 215-225 (2021), DOI `10.1016/j.actaastro.2021.05.012`.
- Measured capillary wavelengths at `120`, `60`, and `25 kHz` agree with theory and decrease with increasing frequency.
- Partial and irregular wave coverage is observed during film spreading.
- Simulations show that frequency, vibration amplitude, and film thickness govern stable wave formation and atomization.
- The system accelerates charged liquid droplets: it is propellant-based electric propulsion, not reactionless propulsion or gravity control.
