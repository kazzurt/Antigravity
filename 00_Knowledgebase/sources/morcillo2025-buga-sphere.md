# Morcillo — A Unified Framework for the Buga Sphere

## One-line thesis

The manuscript assumes reported mass, motion, thermal, and imaging anomalies are accurate, then fits them with engineered negative-mass inclusions governed by a “topo-temporal” axiom.

## Core content

- Infers an 8.1 kg negative-mass inventory from an asserted 10.0 kg to 1.9 kg mass chronology.
- Calculates an 81% “inertial shielding” factor, microscopic core counts, tiny dipole thrust, and approximately 100 W endothermic behavior.
- Multiplies self-assigned confidence factors to report roughly 85% plausibility.
- Proposes resonance mapping, analogue cavities, timing probes, and nanofabrication studies.

## Why it matters here

The paper is useful mainly as a list of extraordinary claims and suggested measurements. A credible program must first establish custody, instrumentation, raw data, material composition, and reproducible mass/thermal behavior before fitting exotic theory.

## Evidence assessment

Speculative SSRN manuscript. Key premises are sourced largely to media reports or materials that do not establish controlled measurements. The numerical model is conditional on those premises and is not independent validation; multiplying subjective confidence factors is not a defensible likelihood calculation.
