## One-line thesis

PURSUE 04 broadens the public UAP archive, but the summarized clips generally lack the range, telemetry, calibration, synchronized sensors, and custody history needed to infer extraordinary kinematics or propulsion.

## Key content

- Inventory and descriptions for 19 videos, including PR100–PR116, PR024, and PR030.
- A geometry reminder that apparent angular motion cannot yield speed without range.
- Identification of missing metadata needed for size, speed, trajectory, and sensor interpretation.
- Triage of PR100 as most useful for follow-up, PR104 as an optics/processing caution, PR112 as strong testimony but weak measurement, PR116 as a likely ordinary control, and PR106/109/113 as weak-custody records for quantitative analysis.
- Conclusion that the public clips do not unambiguously show extreme acceleration, hypersonic no-boom flight, hovering without lift, transmedium passage, resolved structure, or impossible trajectories.
