## Working synthesis

The target remains an **engineered local inertial frame / protected worldtube**: a bounded interior is nearly geodesic and low-tidal while the complete source–shell–payload system changes trajectory relative to its surroundings.

Two new reports resolve the superconducting analogy and the first worldtube source gate:

- [London–GEM Eigenmode Theorem and Finite-Boundary Analysis](../01_Reports-and-Syntheses/London-GEM-Eigenmode-Theorem-and-Boundary-Analysis-2026.md)

The London–GEM equations have a rank-one response matrix because one Cooper-pair current sources both fields. They possess one London-screened mixed eigenmode and one exactly harmonic eigenmode. Generic simultaneous magnetic and gravitomagnetic expulsion does not follow. Removing the harmonic mode for Earth's frame-dragging scale would require a boundary magnetic field of order `8 × 10^17 T`, and the model does not screen the stationary gravitoelectric/Newtonian field. Ordinary single-condensate gravitomagnetism is therefore closed as a gain mechanism.

The companion [rotating-superconductor response derivation](../DERIVATION_PACKAGE.md) closes the unresolved ionic-current step. At fixed rigid rotation, the superconductivity-induced equilibrium bulk ionic current is exactly zero; the lattice's laboratory mass current is ordinary rigid-body motion already present in the normal state. A two-component stiffness matrix does allow a charge-neutral relative mass-current mode, but only after adding an independent coherent component. In the neutral-component limit, reproducing Tate's `92 ppm` comparison requires a cross-stiffness ratio `ρ_sχ/ρ_s = 9.2 × 10^-5` and stability requires `ρ_χ/ρ_s > 8.46 × 10^-9`. This is a coefficient target, not evidence for a niobium mode.

The worldtube inverse design shows why a tiny metric amplitude is still difficult to engineer. For the `1 g`, `10 m` reference case,

`gL/c² = 1.09 × 10^-15`,

and Earth's natural tide is already far below the `1%` limit. The hard requirement is terminating the lapse gradient at a finite boundary. The least-geometry, flat-spatial-slice target requires anisotropic shell stress of order

`c²g/(8πGd) = 5.25 × 10^27 Pa`

for a `0.10 m` wall. A quiet-exterior Newtonian representation instead requires paired positive and negative effective mass with an absolute inventory around `1.9 × 10^14 kg` for a 5 m radius cavity.

A scalar-controlled dRGT-like in-medium metric mass has a nonempty linear attenuation window: `99%` attenuation through `0.10 m` requires `m_g c² ≳ 9.1 μeV`, and the homogeneous-phase `Λ₃` scale is not the immediate obstruction. The craft branch nevertheless fails Gate 0 because no known material supplies the reference/Stückelberg sector or Planck-normalized portal, no stable finite-shell solution has been demonstrated, the evaluated mass potential backreacts at roughly `10^16 J/m³`, and a closed compact switch does not provide the external support needed to change the complete system's trajectory.

The new lead is an **externally supported universal worldtube / field-sail hybrid**. An Earth-linked, remote, ambient, or radiated field must supply direction and opposite momentum; a craft-local sector must then distribute the response through one physical metric so the hull, occupants, payload, clocks, and light share it. This is more constrained than a hull-only fifth-force sail and more conservation-complete than a closed shielding shell.

## Frozen results

1. A true cancellation of the physical lapse gradient in a cavity stationary relative to Earth would make fixed interior worldlines weightless/levitated in that idealization; it is not ordinary free fall through an unmodified Earth field.
2. Interior cancellation does not by itself prove that the complete shell, source, and payload hover. Whole-system motion requires an external stress connection, momentum flux, altered passive charge, exotic monopole/stress, or global boundary reservoir.
3. Static hover can require negligible steady mechanical power while still requiring a source, formation energy, field stress, and an opposite reaction.
4. Deep-space acceleration cannot reuse Earth as the reservoir. An external beam/background/source or emitted momentum must be specified separately.
5. One material current can London-screen at most one independent field combination. Geometry does not remove the remaining harmonic mode.
6. A formal penetration length is not a source law. Any proposed gravitational susceptibility must be derived from a stable covariant action and microscopic operator.
