# Relationship Map

## Relationship vocabulary

Use these typed relationships when extending this map:

- `exhibits`: case -> candidate capability
- `explains`: mechanism -> capability
- `requires`: capability or mechanism -> physical requirement
- `supports`: source or experiment -> claim or mechanism ingredient
- `constrains`: source, null result, or theorem -> mechanism
- `contradicts`: source or result -> claim
- `tests`: experiment -> mechanism ingredient
- `inspired_by`: thread or idea -> source

These links describe knowledge structure, not equal evidence. The canonical narrative pages remain the source of context until an automated graph helper is installed.

## Important non-links

- Standard gravitomagnetism does **not** validate Hively-Loebl scalar waves, the Buga negative-mass model, or Ferent's superluminal gravitons.
- A Maxwell-like mathematical form does **not** imply electromagnetic apparatus can generate gravity at comparable strength.
- Gravitational redshift does **not** by itself imply that a magnetic or electric field can exchange energy with a local gravitational reservoir according to a dimension-matched algebraic law.
- Superconducting phase coherence does **not** by itself imply coherent ionic rotation, enhanced source stress-energy, or large gravitomagnetic permeability.
- A London-moment residual does **not** by itself distinguish omitted condensed-matter response from an external gravitomagnetic field.
- A superconductor WEP/free-fall null does **not** by itself exclude source-side generation, dynamic couplings, or shielding claims acting on other bodies; surviving it lends those claims no support.
- A cavity-induced change in superfluid density does **not** imply a change in weight, inertia, stress-energy coupling, or gravitational permeability.
- A high-current pulse correlated with accelerometer ringing does **not** establish net impulse or reactionless propulsion.
- Phonon angular momentum does **not** by itself imply a DC ionic mass current or coherent coupling to gravity.
- A government research award does **not** establish that the proposed effect was observed.
- An unfinished experiment is neither a positive result nor a decisive null.
- Internal numerical fit does **not** convert unverified observations into evidence for an exotic mechanism.
- Unresolved government status does **not** mean extraordinary kinematics were measured.
- Frame decoupling does **not** automatically imply FTL; causal-structure control adds separate requirements.
- A universal horizon in a three-dimensional Lorentz-violating black-hole solution does **not** provide a four-dimensional, traversable, steerable, or propulsion-capable worldtube.
- Auxiliary-field corrections to Einstein's equations do **not** by themselves identify an actuator, source magnitude, or momentum reservoir.
- A visually striking UAP clip does **not** exhibit a capability until its geometry and sensor behavior support that inference.
