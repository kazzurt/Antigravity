## Current leading thread — independently reassess it

The present lead is superconducting coherence and gravity through gravitomagnetism, originating in Ning Li and Douglas Torr's work. The current knowledgebase believes:

1. Li's weak-field canonical-momentum coupling is a legitimate starting point.
2. Her large effect depends on an unsupported transfer of superconducting phase quantization to lattice ions, a nonzero-circulation argument that does not follow, and an assumed material gravitomagnetic permeability whose assigned magnitude varies drastically across the papers.
3. Her useful surviving intuition is kinematic: oppositely charged currents can cancel electrically while their positive mass currents add, if a real second mobile coherent component exists.
4. Tate's rotating-niobium London-moment result is structurally relevant because it measures condensate response relative to a rotating lattice. It is not automatically a direct free-pair mass measurement or a gravitational detection.
5. A `92 ppm` residual, parameterized as omitted ionic inertia, corresponds to only about `1.1 × 10^-9` of a niobium atom's inertia per Cooper pair. This makes weak entrainment numerically interesting but is not evidence of co-moving ions.
6. Hoang et al. later reported a smaller niobium residual near `18 ± 2.1 ppm` within a six-material study, but its evidence package needs close scrutiny before it is treated as independent confirmation.
7. The best modern replacement for an assumed scalar `μ_g` is a causal, frequency- and wavevector-dependent matter/stress-energy response kernel.
8. The strongest clean realization of Li's counterflow intuition is a genuine multicomponent quantum fluid with oppositely charged mobile condensates. Other partial ingredients include axial/chiral phonons, Carlson–Goldman modes, Leggett modes, entrainment, exciton counterflow, and spin counterflow.
9. No known niobium state has yet been shown to combine charge cancellation, large surviving mass/angular-momentum response, condensate locking, and anomalous coupling to gravity.

Audit all nine conclusions rather than merely repeating them.
