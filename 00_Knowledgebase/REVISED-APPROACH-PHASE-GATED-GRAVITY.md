## Executive decision

The project will continue to work from its adopted premise that useful gravitational or inertial-frame modulation is physically possible. The premise is a search directive, not evidence for any particular mechanism.

The Ning Li, Tate/London-moment, intrinsic-spin, and Alzofon investigations have clarified where the missing physics cannot reside. Ordinary mass current, intrinsic angular momentum, superconducting charge current, and laboratory electromagnetic stress-energy all gravitate in established theory, but their conventional fields are far too small for craft-scale control. Quantum coherence can organize a source and provide a sharp state switch; it does not, by itself, multiply the gravitational coupling.

The revised lead is therefore not “make a larger gravitomagnetic source.” It is:

> **Find a covariant, conservation-complete mechanism in which an electromagnetic or material-state control changes the coupling, propagation, screening, or boundary conditions of a universally acting field.**

The next architecture audited was a **phase-gated scalar–tensor field**. In this architecture, electromagnetism supplies or modulates the drive, a material order parameter gates the field locally, and the resulting field acts on ordinary matter through an effective metric or nearly universal coupling.

The completed [requirements-first scalar report](../01_Reports-and-Syntheses/Phase-Gated-Scalar-Tensor-Gravity-Control-Research-Report-2026.md) found a coherent action and a nonempty laboratory mass-wall window, but no craft-scale parameter window. The exact failure is the conjunction of source strength, selective screening, universal craft response, bounded low-tide geometry, and an external momentum reservoir. This is now a constrained laboratory hypothesis, not the project's craft lead.

The subsequent [London–GEM eigenmode theorem](../01_Reports-and-Syntheses/London-GEM-Eigenmode-Theorem-and-Boundary-Analysis-2026.md) and [worldtube/metric-Higgs Gate 0 report](../01_Reports-and-Syntheses/Worldtube-Source-Theorem-and-Phase-Localized-Metric-Higgs-Gate-0-2026.md) sharpen the decision. A single condensate has one screened mixed mode and one harmonic mode; generic gravitomagnetic expulsion does not occur. A physical metric-mass shell has a formal micro-eV attenuation window, but the reference worldtube requires approximately `5 × 10^27 Pa` shell stress or paired positive/negative effective mass at the `10^14 kg` scale. No known material supplies that portal or source, and a closed shell does not establish whole-craft support.

## Why the earlier lead was demoted

1. **The London moment is electromagnetic.** Rotation of a superconductor generates the ordinary London magnetic field through condensate–lattice electrodynamics. Applying a much larger magnetic field changes screening currents, fluxoid number, vortices, and eventually the superconducting state; it does not imply a corresponding gravitomagnetic field.
2. **Fluxoid quantization is not gravitational-flux conversion.** Writing electromagnetic and weak gravitational vector potentials in a canonical momentum does not create a conservation law that trades magnetic flux for gravitational flux.
3. **The conventional source scale is fatal for propulsion.** Closing the complete stress-energy budget removes hidden double counting but does not produce the many orders of magnitude required for useful frame control.
4. **Spin does not supply automatic gain.** Intrinsic spin contributes angular momentum and stress-energy, but it is not a literal classical circulation of rest mass. A useful anomalous spin–gravity law would itself be new physics and must be written explicitly.
5. **The Tate residual is high-quality metrology, not yet gravity evidence.** Resolving it remains worthwhile for condensed-matter closure and experimental calibration, but even a real unconventional matter response would still require a separate source-to-field enhancement law.

## The new physical hypothesis

A useful minimal scaffold contains four logically distinct functions:

| Function | Candidate mathematical role | Physical purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Universal response | Matter sees `g̃_μν = A²(φ) g_μν`, or an equivalent metric coupling | Accelerate the hull, instruments, and occupants together |
| Field dynamics | `−½ Z(φ)(∂φ)² − V(φ)` and possibly `F(φ)R` | Define range, stability, propagation, and relation to metric gravity |
| Electromagnetic control | A term such as `B_F(φ) F_μνF^μν` | Permit magnetic, electric, optical, or resonant pumping |
| Material-state gate | A term such as `λ f(Ψ†Ψ, φ)` | Change the field's local mass, coupling, or boundary condition across a phase transition |

For a simple scalar coupling, the nonrelativistic anomalous acceleration is schematically

`a_φ ≈ −c² ∇ln A(φ)`.

A superconducting order parameter `|Ψ|²`, magnetic order, spin polarization, ferroelectric order, strain field, or another collective variable could in principle modify an effective field mass,

`m_eff² = m_0² + α ρ + λ |Ψ|² + …`,

thereby changing penetration or screening. No useful value of `λ` is established. The audit found a classical boundary window—approximately `m_out ≲ 2 × 10⁻⁸ eV` and `m_wall ≳ 2 × 10⁻⁵ eV` for a `10 m` interior and `1 cm` wall—but the portal must be new, gauge-invariant, radiatively controlled, and measured. The window permits a transmission experiment; it does not supply a `1 g` source or net propulsion.

## The crucial gain idea

The control input does not necessarily have to supply the full field energy. It could act as a gate on a pre-existing field or reservoir, analogous to a transistor controlling a larger energy flow. This does not relax conservation laws:

- energy must be stored in or supplied by the field, environment, source mass, or background;
- momentum must be exchanged with Earth, an external field, emitted radiation, or another identifiable reservoir;
- a closed craft cannot accelerate its center of mass using only internal static forces;
- true cancellation of the physical lapse gradient in a cavity held stationary relative to Earth would produce local weightlessness/levitation, not free fall through an unmodified field; however, cavity cancellation does not by itself support the complete source–shell–payload system.

The project must therefore identify the **reservoir and momentum sink** for every candidate, not merely the local actuator.

## Craft-level target

The target remains an engineered local worldtube: a bounded interior that is nearly geodesic and low-tidal while its external trajectory changes. For orientation, a uniform `1 g` acceleration across a `10 m` cabin requires only a weak-field metric difference of order

`Δh_00 ≈ 2 g L / c² ≈ 2.2 × 10⁻¹⁵`.

The difficulty is not necessarily a large dimensionless metric amplitude. It is producing the correct spatial gradient and boundary curvature, maintaining a low-tidal interior, coupling nearly universally to all onboard matter, and closing the energy–momentum budget.

Ordinary London gravitomagnetism and applied-field stress-energy remain mandatory baselines, not candidate gain mechanisms.

The conventional baseline now includes the corrected neutral-current sign rule: for opposite charges, charge cancellation makes co-directed positive mass currents add; it does not require strong mass asymmetry. This keeps excitonic center-of-mass flow available as a matter-response template while leaving the gravitational-coupling and magnitude problems untouched.

## Frozen conclusions

- Do not reintroduce a large scalar gravitational permeability without a covariant action and a conserved source.
- Do not count magnetic, lattice, condensate, or spin angular momentum twice.
- Do not use “coherence” as a gain mechanism without naming the controlled variable and coupling.
- Do not confuse an analog or synthetic metric with curvature of physical spacetime.
- Do not call a composition-dependent fifth force craft-compatible unless occupant, payload, and hull response are addressed.
- Do not call a mechanism propulsion-complete without an external momentum exchange.
- Treat simple chameleon and symmetron models as instructive templates, not as surviving solutions; laboratory and equivalence-principle constraints must be applied quantitatively.

## Open questions

1. Which explicit external mediator can carry momentum from a remote/Earth source while all onboard matter responds through one physical metric?
2. Can the mediator be strong in a prepared source–sail channel without violating WEP, inverse-square, PPN, clock, radiation, and astrophysical bounds?
3. Can a phase-gated shell change an externally imposed field transfer function without hiding the effect in an unexplained susceptibility?
4. Can the nested-shell experiment resolve a state-dependent fraction of a modulated source-mass field with two independent detector technologies?
5. Can a multicomponent condensate produce a rank-two invariant response, and does either mode affect the gravitoelectric metric?
6. Which source sector can approach the inverse-designed `10^27 Pa` stress requirement with a stable sign and a complete reaction channel?
