Ratings are local triage categories, not journal rankings. `A` means the source is suitable for standard theoretical background; `E` means it should be cited only as the origin of a claim unless independently substantiated.

## Dual-axis use

The rating above measures independent evidentiary strength. Project relevance is recorded separately in source digests and may be high even for a constrained or speculative source. Conversely, an established theory source may have little direct actuator relevance.
