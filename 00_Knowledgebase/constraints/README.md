# Constraints and Negative Results

Negative results are design constraints, not automatic rejection of the project premise. This register prevents repeatedly pursuing bridges already ruled out at a relevant scale or under a stated assumption.

## Constraint families

| Family | What must be recorded | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Conservation | Complete momentum and energy accounting; open versus closed system | Eliminates hidden reaction paths and mechanical ratchets |
| Scaling | Required versus achievable field, energy, stress, force, or curvature | Prevents analogy from substituting for magnitude |
| Equivalence | Whether gravitational and inertial responses change together | Constrains inertial-modification proposals |
| Tidal stress | Interior gradients, not only center-of-mass acceleration | Determines occupant and craft survivability |
| Energy conditions | Observer-robust stress-energy classification | Constrains classical warp constructions |
| Stability and horizons | Startup, steering, communication, and shutdown | Separates mathematical geometry from controllable transport |
| Measurement artifacts | Thermal, EM, acoustic, vibration, buoyancy, cable, software, and optics | Prevents false propulsion and false kinematics |
| Chain of custody | Raw data, processing, calibration, and provenance | Determines whether quantitative analysis is admissible |

## Interpretation rule

A constraint is scoped. For example, a null result in one cavity geometry constrains that apparatus, sensitivity, and coupling law; it does not prove that every possible frame-control mechanism is impossible. Conversely, the project premise does not permit ignoring a constraint that directly applies.

## Current high-value constraints

- The September [two-metric Gate 0](../../01_Reports-and-Syntheses/Tensor-Mode-Transformer-Gate-0-2026.md) shows that a scalar multiplying the tuned interaction potential cannot rotate the mass eigenvectors on the fixed proportional background with fixed kinetic coefficients. The common field remains unchanged in that linear diagnostic; this does not exclude other supported backgrounds or theories.
- The September [Tate finite-ring audit](../../01_Reports-and-Syntheses/Tate-Finite-Ring-and-Measurement-Audit-2026.md) distinguishes bulk-field and winding-spacing coefficients. The neutral `1+x` spacing requires fixed neutral winding; repeated spacing measurements in one unchanged geometry cannot independently identify entrainment, area, and surface contributions.

- Closed-system internal mechanical motion cannot produce net center-of-mass acceleration.
- Standard laboratory gravitomagnetic fields are far below propulsion-relevant scale.
- JASON JSR-08-506 finds the Li-Baker HFGW detector sensitivity wrong by many orders (including a homodyne estimate over 20 orders too optimistic) and foreseeable laboratory HFGW communications, imaging, and propulsion infeasible; new proposals must close the cited source and detector budgets explicitly.
- Li's static YBCO experiment constrains a gravity change in its configuration below `2 × 10^-8 g`; it does not by itself constrain every dynamic mixed response.
- The Eöt-Wash cryogenic WEP test (Ross et al., PRD 111, L021101 (2025)) bounds anomalous solar free fall of superconducting niobium at `2.0 × 10^-9` and of Cooper pairs at `9.2 × 10^-4`; surviving superconductor-gravity proposals must be source-side, dynamic/AC, vertical-only, or below these scales.
- Meissner screening cannot be used as a near-zero constitutive permeability denominator without a microscopic and gauge-consistent derivation.
- A claimed coherent mass current must identify the physical carrier, angular momentum, energy, and complete stress-energy; phase coherence alone is insufficient.
- Inan's standard-framework calculation gives no isolated gravito-Meissner effect and negligible terrestrial corrections; the project boundary audit further shows that generic simultaneous `B`/`B_G` decay cannot be assumed from a particular solution.
- Ummarino-Gallerati's favorable TDGL response is only order `10^-7` on a roughly nanosecond scale near `T_c` before further material suppression, and remains unmeasured.
- Keren et al. demonstrate that a resonant cavity can alter superfluid density. This validates a material-state gate, not a gravitational portal, source, or force.
- Public UAP video without range cannot establish true speed through screen motion alone.
- Processed or altered video without transformation history is unsuitable for precision photogrammetry.
- Classical accelerating or superluminal warp models face energy-condition, source, and control obstructions documented in the synthesis reports.
- A modified-gravity proposal must identify its broken/preserved symmetries, propagating modes, stability and strong-coupling regime, matter coupling, and recovery of GR before actuator scaling is considered.
- In Vernieri's auxiliary-field class, eliminating non-dynamical fields generically introduces higher derivatives of matter. Sharp density transitions yield strong local bounds (`λ_β ≪ n^-1/2 mm` in the thesis's conservative meter/micron example), so an interface is a constraint amplifier unless a controlled operating window is derived.
- Resonator measurements require predeclared artifact budgets and orthogonal observables.
- Kiloampere pulsed-emitter and claimed-levitation tests require an isolated force/impulse measurement plus matched cable, electromagnetic, acoustic, airflow, structural-ringdown, sensor-transfer-function, thermal, and blind-sequence controls; a first accelerometer lobe is not sufficient.
