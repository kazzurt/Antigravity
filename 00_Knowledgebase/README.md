# Antigravity Research Knowledge Base

This knowledge base is the structured research map for the Antigravity project. It began as the digest of files organized from the project root on 2026-07-15 and now also carries the project's mission, assumption ladder, capability model, mechanism portfolio, UAP case register, constraints, and ranked investigation threads.

## Mission and working premise

The project adopts as a research premise that at least one engineered technology can separate a craft's local inertial experience from its motion relative to its surroundings, and that some persistent UAP reports are imperfect observations of consequences of such a system.

The preferred core model is an **engineered local inertial frame / protected worldtube**: the interior remains near-geodesic and low-tidal while the bounded region changes motion relative to the exterior. Extreme maneuvering and transmedium behavior are downstream capability hypotheses. FTL is a separate program-horizon hypothesis with additional causal and global-geometry requirements.

This premise guides discovery; it does not change the evidence rating of a source.

## Executive synthesis

The source collection separates into five very different evidence classes:

1. **General-relativistic gravitomagnetism.** Mashhoon, Gronwald, and Lichtenegger review observable frame-dragging and clock effects; Costa and Herdeiro give the most rigorous gravito-electromagnetic analogy in the set by formulating it with tidal tensors; Malekolkalami and Farhoudi analyze the recurring factor-of-two issue in simplified derivations. These sources are useful for definitions, limits, and testable weak-field effects. They do not provide an antigravity or propulsion mechanism.
2. **Superconductivity and gravity.** Ning Li and Douglas Torr's 1991–1993 program is the collection's most explicit attempt to turn quantum coherence into a large gravitomagnetic material response. Its weak-field canonical-momentum starting point is legitimate, but its amplification depends on unsupported lattice-ion circulation and permeability assumptions. The completed [rotating-superconductor derivation](../DERIVATION_PACKAGE.md) now makes that failure precise: at fixed rigid rotation, ordinary superconductivity adds no equilibrium bulk ionic current, while a surviving neutral mass-current mode requires a genuinely independent second coherent component. Li's 1997 static YBCO test was null below `2 × 10^-8 g`; the intended later NASA integrated experiment was not completed.
3. **Alternative field frameworks.** Vernieri supplies the collection's most disciplined modified-gravity map: Hořava gravity and non-dynamical auxiliary fields are tested through symmetry, degree-of-freedom, stability, causal-structure, and local-limit questions. Dunning-Davies, Stepanovic, Okunev, and Hively-Loebl propose modified Maxwell-like or unified field equations. They contain reusable mathematical questions and sometimes testable predictions, but they depart from standard theory and do not establish superiority through independent, high-quality experimental validation.
4. **Extraordinary claims.** Morcillo's Buga Sphere model and Ferent's personal quantum-gravity framework assume or assert phenomena that are not established by the documents' evidence. They should be treated as hypothesis or claim inventories, not as confirmed physics.
5. **UAP case synthesis.** The modern UAP report and PURSUE 04 summary organize witness, sensor, and government-release material. They define candidate capabilities and decisive data requirements, but public video without calibrated geometry does not establish exotic performance.

The strongest source-audit conclusion remains: **none of the current files independently demonstrates controllable gravity, inertial-mass reduction, negative mass, or reactionless propulsion.** Under the new mission, that is a constraint baseline rather than the project's endpoint. Sources are mined for necessary physics, control variables, scaling gaps, predicted signatures, and decisive tests.

The current strategic lead is an **externally supported universal worldtube / field-sail architecture**. The assessed scalar and metric-mass shell models did not establish a craft window. Material order remains a possible gate, not an established source of gravitational amplification. [Current Best Path](CURRENT-BEST-PATH.md) owns the architecture ranking; [Next-Conversation Brief](NEXT-CONVERSATION-BRIEF.md) owns current execution and deliverable status.

**Completed research pass, 2026-09-04:** the static two-metric calculation closes scalar-prefactor common-mode conversion in the tested model; the Tate audit completes a conditional finite-ring/winding mapping and identifies the missing empirical inputs. Both have reproducible calculations and internal review. Read the [parallel research review](../01_Reports-and-Syntheses/Parallel-Research-Review-2026-09-04.md) and [current handoff](NEXT-CONVERSATION-BRIEF.md). No gravity anomaly or material portal is established by this work.

The knowledgebase therefore maintains two views:

- **Inside the premise:** what a source contributes to reverse-engineering frame decoupling.
- **External evidence:** what the source independently establishes and what remains unresolved.

## Scope and conventions

- The original PDFs remain authoritative for equations, figures, and layout.
- Co-located Markdown twins are machine-extracted text for search and agent access.
- Evidence ratings describe support visible in this collection; they are not universal verdicts on a field.
- Premise status and evidence status are always recorded separately.
- A null result constrains a stated apparatus, scale, or mechanism; it is not silently generalized to every possible implementation.
- A UAP case enters the capability model only to the extent allowed by its range, geometry, calibration, sensor coverage, and chain of custody.
- “Undated” means the PDF does not expose a reliable publication year. The Ferent document is internally inconsistent about date.
