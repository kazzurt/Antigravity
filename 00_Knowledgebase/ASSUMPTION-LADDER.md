# Assumption Ladder

This ladder decomposes the project's destination into dependencies. A source can support, constrain, or illuminate one level without establishing the levels above it.

| Level | Proposition | Project status | External-evidence status | Main discriminator |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P0** | Some UAP reports contain a real anomalous-performance signal rather than only reporting noise. | Adopted premise | Plausible for a residual subset; case dependent | Calibrated multi-sensor records with complete geometry and chain of custody |
| **P1** | At least one residual phenomenon is engineered nonconventional technology. | Adopted premise | Not established by the public collection | Resolved structure, repeatable behavior, or signatures excluding ordinary objects and sensor effects |
| **P2** | The technology separates the craft's local inertial experience from its externally observed motion. | Core working model | Not directly measured publicly | Extreme coordinate acceleration paired with evidence of low proper acceleration and controlled tidal gradients |
| **P3** | Separation is achieved through controllable spacetime geometry, inertial coupling, or gravitational constitutive behavior. | Mechanism class to discover | Open | A quantitative field model with conservation laws, a source term, scaling, and distinctive observables |
| **P4** | The same boundary-field system enables transmedium travel or strong suppression of environmental coupling. | Strong extension | Public evidence is presently weak | Instrumented medium transition with continuous trajectory, pressure, thermal, acoustic, and field data |
| **P5** | The system permits effective FTL travel or global causal-structure engineering. | Program-horizon hypothesis | No public demonstration | A stable sourceable geometry that addresses horizons, causality, energy conditions, and control |

## Dependency rules

- P2 is the project's preferred explanation for P0–P1, but any case can fail to support P2 without threatening the premise globally.
- P3 is deliberately plural: local metric engineering, modified inertial coupling, vacuum response, or another field description remain candidates.
- P4 must not be inferred from a video merely showing an object near water.
- P5 is not a synonym for rapid acceleration. It has additional causal and global-geometry requirements.

## Status vocabulary

Use these labels in future notes:

- `adopted-premise` — fixed for purposes of exploration;
- `working-model` — preferred explanation being refined;
- `candidate-mechanism` — a proposed route, not selected truth;
- `supported-in-source` — directly supported by the cited source;
- `project-inference` — synthesis made by this knowledgebase;
- `speculative-extension` — useful horizon with unresolved dependencies;
- `constrained` — conflicts with a quantitative requirement or reliable null result;
- `unresolved` — evidence is insufficient to decide.

## Anti-contamination rule

Premise status and evidence status are separate fields. An adopted premise determines what the project searches for; it does not raise a document's evidence rating.
