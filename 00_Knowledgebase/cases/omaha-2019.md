# Watch-List Case — USS Omaha Sphere (2019)

## Source basis

This node digests the local [UAP evidence report](../../01_Reports-and-Syntheses/UAP-Advanced-Capabilities-Deep-Research-Report.md). The report did not recover an official public technical packet or raw telemetry sufficient to reconstruct a medium transition.

## Claim of interest

Public discussion associates a spherical object near USS Omaha with a possible air-to-water transition.

## Capability implication if verified

A continuous controlled transition could constrain drag, cavitation, pressure, thermal, acoustic, and boundary-field models. It would be highly relevant to P4 and the transmedium-boundary thread.

## Present evidentiary limit

The public package summarized locally does not establish a continuous calibrated 3D path through the air-water boundary. An object disappearing at or near the horizon/water line is not enough.

## Strongest controls

- line-of-sight loss or sensor handoff;
- ordinary water entry;
- range and horizon ambiguity;
- unrelated contacts being narratively combined.

## Decisive missing evidence

- continuous synchronized radar, EO/IR, and sonar track;
- known range, altitude, bearing, and platform state;
- splash, cavity, acoustic, and thermal observations;
- raw files and complete custody history.

## Assumption-ladder mapping

- **P0:** watch-list relevance.
- **P1–P2:** not established.
- **P4:** motivating claim, presently unsupported by the required public measurements.
- **P5:** no bearing.

## Case status

Watch list only; do not use as a pillar for transmedium capability until a continuous instrumented transition is available.
