# Case Cluster — USS Theodore Roosevelt / Virginia Beach (2014–2015)

## Source basis

This node digests the local [UAP evidence report](../../01_Reports-and-Syntheses/UAP-Advanced-Capabilities-Deep-Research-Report.md), including the recurring encounter pattern, GIMBAL, and GOFAST.

## Direct public record summarized locally

- Ryan Graves testified that unknown tracks became recurrent after a radar upgrade and were later corroborated with infrared sensors.
- A near-collision report described a dark cube inside a clear sphere near a training-area entry point.
- GIMBAL and GOFAST are authenticated Navy videos.
- Public AARO analysis explains GOFAST's apparent high speed through motion parallax and wind-consistent motion.

## Cluster rule

The recurring encounters, GIMBAL, and GOFAST are related by operating period and reporting community but are not automatically the same object or mechanism. The resolved geometry of one clip does not resolve the whole cluster; the cluster does not make every clip anomalous.

## Candidate capabilities

- persistence and station-keeping in strong winds;
- extended endurance;
- low observable lift or propulsion;
- occasional reported acceleration;
- possible variable or unusual apparent morphology.

## Frame-decoupling interpretation

Persistence and station-keeping could be consistent with local trajectory/environmental control, but the public data do not force that interpretation. The cluster is most useful for defining repeated-observation and aviation-safety requirements.

## Strongest controls

- sensor behavior after system upgrades;
- parallax and unknown range;
- distant aircraft or other ordinary targets;
- heterogeneity across events grouped under one narrative;
- optical/gimbal effects affecting apparent rotation.

## Decisive missing evidence

- raw radar and IR records for the recurring encounters;
- target range and synchronized platform geometry;
- event-by-event identifiers preventing narrative aggregation;
- wind, endurance, and trajectory reconstructions for the cube/sphere report.

## Assumption-ladder mapping

- **P0:** supports a persistent residual-observation problem.
- **P1:** not established for the cluster.
- **P2:** candidate interpretation only for events surviving geometry reconstruction.
- **P3–P5:** not discriminated.

## Case status

Important pattern case; GOFAST down-ranked as a performance case, GIMBAL unresolved-but-mundane-plausible, broader recurring reports unresolved.
