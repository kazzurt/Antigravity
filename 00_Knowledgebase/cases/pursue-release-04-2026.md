## Direct contribution to the frame-decoupling program

This release does **not** currently establish P2 frame decoupling. It contributes three things:

1. **A case-selection filter.** Favor synchronized multi-sensor cases with known range and an unbroken chain of custody.
2. **A measurement specification.** It identifies the telemetry required to distinguish extraordinary trajectory from small nearby objects or sensor behavior.
3. **A signature-design prompt.** A frame-decoupling theory must predict what calibrated radar, EO/IR, acoustic, atmospheric, and transmedium sensors should observe.

## Critical missing data

- UTC time and precise coordinates;
- sensor platform, altitude, speed, and heading;
- sensor orientation, calibrated field of view, spectral band, native resolution, and frame rate;
- target range, slant range, range rate, and full 3D track;
- synchronized radar or second-sensor observations;
- raw uncompressed files and complete processing history;
- atmospheric and wind profiles;
- full chain of custody.

Without range \(R\), angular rate \(\omega\) yields only an undetermined transverse speed:

\[
v_{\perp}=R\omega.
\]

## Strongest conventional alternatives

- balloons, birds, drones, aircraft, and surveillance objects;
- parallax and unknown-range geometry;
- infrared point-spread and saturation effects;
- image processing, zoom, stabilization, replay, or compression;
- collection bias around military operating areas;
- incomplete files that remain unresolved because identification is impossible after the fact.

These alternatives are control hypotheses. Removing a weak case improves the project by preventing false capability requirements.

## Decisive next evidence

A high-priority future case should include simultaneous radar, EO/IR, and visible observations; known range and platform state; full three-dimensional trajectory; raw files; atmospheric conditions; documented custody; and independent technical reconstruction.

## Assumption-ladder mapping

- **P0:** relevant as a pool from which genuine residual events may emerge.
- **P1:** not independently established by the public material summarized here.
- **P2–P4:** useful for defining required measurements, not yet evidenced.
- **P5:** not addressed.

## Bottom line

PURSUE 04 is strategically important as a growing public archive, but its immediate research value is methodological. It tells this project what data a frame-decoupling case must contain and which visually compelling but underdetermined patterns should be down-ranked.
