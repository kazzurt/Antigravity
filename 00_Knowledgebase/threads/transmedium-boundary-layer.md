# Thread — Transmedium Boundary Layer

## Objective

Translate claimed air-water continuity into competing physical models and a decisive multi-sensor measurement plan.

## Capability coverage

Drag suppression, pressure management, cavitation control, thermal protection, shock/acoustic suppression, and sensor-signature modification.

## Models to compare

- ordinary small object plus incomplete geometry;
- supercavitation or gas-envelope control;
- MHD/plasma boundary control;
- hydrophobic or structured-surface effects;
- frame-coupled reduction of environmental interaction.

## Work packages

1. Predict splash, cavity, pressure, acoustic, thermal, optical, RF, radar, and sonar signatures for each model.
2. State energy and momentum transfer into the two media.
3. Identify the minimum continuous trajectory required to establish a transition.
4. Separate vehicle shape from optical/plasma point-spread effects.
5. Design a synchronized sensor package and metadata schema.

## Decisive failure conditions

- no continuous calibrated air-water track;
- signatures are fully explained by ordinary entry, parallax, or sensor handoff;
- proposed envelope cannot meet pressure, power, or heat constraints;
- subsystem model does not integrate with the core inertial mechanism.

## Current evidence position

The supplied PURSUE 04 summary does not contain a demonstrated transmedium case. It informs the measurement plan but not the capability claim.

## Next deliverable

A signature matrix for an instrumented transition, usable to score future UAP cases and small-scale water-entry analog experiments.
