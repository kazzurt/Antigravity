# Thread — Engineered Worldtube

**Updated:** 2026-09-04
**Stage:** requirements, diagnostic inverse design, and the scalar-prefactor static two-metric gate are completed within their stated scopes.

## Objective

Specify the minimum invariant conditions for a controllable region with a low-proper-acceleration, low-tidal interior and a changing external trajectory.

## Capability coverage

Extreme maneuvering, occupant protection, hovering, signature control, transmedium integration, and a possible later route to FTL.

## Mechanisms compared

- explicit metric engineering;
- modified inertial coupling;
- generalized constitutive or area-metric response;
- vacuum/boundary-mediated geometry control.

## Work packages

1. Define interior proper-acceleration and tidal limits.
2. Define boundary thickness, regularity, and stability requirements.
3. State momentum and energy accounting for acceleration.
4. Identify invariant curvature and stress-energy observables.
5. Map boundary behavior to remote sensor signatures.
6. Compare candidate models against the same requirement set.

## Decisive failure conditions

- no sourceable configuration can meet the interior/boundary requirements;
- acceleration always requires an uncontrolled horizon or singular stress;
- the model's apparent benefit is coordinate-only;
- the mechanism cannot specify conservation closure.
