# Topic — Alternative Electromagnetic and Gravitational Frameworks

## Shared pattern

This collection contains two distinct strata. Vernieri gives a scholarly modified-gravity treatment organized around Lovelock's theorem, explicit symmetry choices, degree-of-freedom counting, and consistency constraints. Dunning-Davies, Hively-Loebl, Stepanovic, Okunev, Monjo, and Zhu begin with a perceived incompleteness or inconsistency in standard field descriptions and add a field, scalar, derivative rule, or geometric interpretation. Their equations are not mutually equivalent, and none is validated by the other documents.

Two recurring failure modes are worth naming, because they let a candidate framework be dismissed cheaply. The first is **assuming the interaction being derived**: Monjo's construction reproduces Maxwell's equations only after the electromagnetic energy is defined in terms of a pre-existing electric potential, which the author concedes. The second is a **probe-dependent metric**: when the perturbation carries the test particle's charge-to-mass ratio (Monjo's `ζ_1m = q₁/m₁`, and Özer's `q/m` term), there is no single geometry for a region containing differently charged probes, and the equivalence-principle structure the framework was built on collapses.

## Comparison

| Framework | Added or changed element | Claimed payoff | Main unresolved issue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vernieri: Hořava and auxiliary-field programs | Preferred foliation/broken full diffeomorphism invariance, or algebraically eliminable non-dynamical fields | Improved UV power counting, explicit causal tests, and a general two-parameter phenomenology | Full quantum consistency and predictivity; four-dimensional empirical viability; strong local matter-gradient constraints |
| Dunning-Davies | Convective derivative and gravitational vortex | Galilean-invariant Maxwell-like gravity | Conflict with Lorentz-covariant foundations; no discriminating test |
| Hively-Loebl EED | Scalar `C` and modified Maxwell equations | Longitudinal waves and revised conservation | Preliminary, non-independent experimental evidence |
| Stepanovic EEM | Equivalent EM potential for gravity | Alternative metrics and motion equations | Limited scope; second-order perihelion prediction (`5/6` of GR) contradicted by measurement |
| Okunev | Radiation/receiver geometry | Unified explanation of several phenomena | Sparse derivation and unsupported instantaneous response |
| Monjo | Electric metric perturbation `A^αβ ≈ 2ζ_1m ζ₂ u^α u^β` added to `η` | Maxwell, Lorentz force, and Dirac/Klein-Gordon EM terms from curvature alone | Presupposes the EM interaction it derives; metric depends on the probe's `q/m`; `Δα/α = −2Δφ` in tension with white-dwarf limits |
| Zhu | Redshift-energy argument plus dimension-setting constants | Large measurable magnetic/electric modification of `g` | No covariant action, gauge-invariant observable, or confirming high-field experiment |

## Research-use rule

Treat these documents as generators of explicit hypotheses. Before importing a formula, record its assumptions, dimensional consistency, covariance properties, conservation laws, propagating degrees of freedom, stability/strong-coupling regime, GR-recovery mechanism, and one observation that would distinguish it from standard theory.
