# Topic — Anomalous Propulsion and Artifact Claims

## Role under the reframed mission

This topic is a fishing ground for capability clues and possible mechanism ingredients. It is not a single evidence bucket. Artifact reports, UAP cases, patents, resonator anomalies, and alternative theories must be linked through explicit requirements rather than shared vocabulary.

## Buga Sphere manuscript

Morcillo's model does not independently establish a negative-mass artifact. It conditions on a reported mass chronology, thermal behavior, motion, and internal structure, then chooses an exotic model that reproduces those inputs. The immediate research need is measurement provenance, not more parameter fitting.

## Extended-electrodynamics relevance

Hively and Loebl provide testable longitudinal-wave claims that are more experimentally concrete than the Buga model, but their preliminary tests require independent replication with strong controls for leakage, common-mode coupling, grounding, antenna near fields, instrumentation artifacts, and data-selection effects.

## High-frequency gravitational waves

Baker's 2005 paper is a useful catalog of proposed HFGW communications, imaging, propulsion, and focused-energy applications. JASON JSR-08-506 is the controlling assessment: it finds the Li-Baker detector sensitivity wrong by many orders, including a homodyne error exceeding 20 orders, and rejects foreseeable artificial generation, detection, and practical applications. A renewed proposal must close those exact quantitative objections rather than cite the application list.

## Pulsed emitters and apparent levitation

Poher's graphite-emitter paper involves high voltage/current, mechanical structure, vibration, acoustic drive, and difficult sensor coupling. Poher explicitly observes mechanical resonances and unknown accelerometer gain but interprets the first waveform lobe as up to `104 g`. The source does not reach a validated net-force measurement. The admission gate is an isolated impulse/force balance with matched electrical, thermal, acoustic, airflow, cable, and vibration controls.

## Minimum evidence ladder

1. Controlled raw measurements with calibrated uncertainty and full provenance.
2. Conventional-cause exclusion designed before data collection.
3. Independent replication by a team without financial or theoretical investment in the claim.
4. Scaling behavior predicted in advance and inconsistent with known couplings.
5. Only then, comparison among exotic mechanisms.

No source ingested in this batch reaches the full ladder.

## UAP release lesson

The PURSUE 04 conversation summary adds a complementary admission gate for remote observations: known range, platform state, calibrated sensor geometry, raw files, synchronized sensors, atmospheric context, and processing history. Without those fields, a clip can motivate a question but cannot define an extreme-acceleration or transmedium requirement quantitatively.

## Dual-use rule

For every anomalous claim, record:

1. the strongest interpretation inside the frame-decoupling premise;
2. the strongest ordinary or measurement-based alternative;
3. the one observation that would best separate them;
4. the mechanism family implicated if the extraordinary interpretation survives.
