# Mechanism — Warp and Causal-Structure Engineering

## Thesis

Effective FTL would arise from controlling global spacetime relationships while maintaining locally causal motion inside a bounded region.

## Project role

This is the P5 destination branch. It is downstream of, but not guaranteed by, a successful subluminal protected-worldtube model.

## Existing anchors

The Rodal synthesis, warp landscape report, and state-of-the-art report document explicit metrics, invariant diagnostics, energy-condition problems, acceleration gaps, and current no-go results.

## Additional requirements beyond frame decoupling

- creation and acceleration of the geometry;
- horizon formation and control;
- stable navigation and communication;
- causal consistency and chronology protection;
- sourceable stress-energy or a validated extension beyond classical GR;
- safe startup, transit, and shutdown.

## Fatal constraints

- a constant-velocity geometry with no self-acceleration mechanism;
- observer-dependent “positive energy” that fails invariant or all-observer tests;
- horizon or instability that prevents control from inside;
- an energy-condition workaround that replaces one inconsistency with another.

Which requirements of a stable subluminal worldtube remain unchanged in the superluminal regime, and which become qualitatively new?
