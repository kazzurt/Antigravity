# Mechanism — Engineered Local Worldtube

## Thesis

A craft occupies a bounded region whose interior remains near-geodesic and low-tidal while the region's relation to the exterior geometry changes.

## Why it leads

This is the cleanest translation of “inertial separation” into a physics target. It can explain why large coordinate acceleration need not imply large occupant proper acceleration and provides a single framework for trajectory, environmental coupling, and possibly later causal-structure engineering.

## Required physics

- a finite interior with bounded proper acceleration and curvature gradients;
- a controllable boundary stress-energy or generalized constitutive response;
- acceleration and steering without hiding momentum exchange;
- stability against collapse, horizons, radiation, and runaway gradients;
- exterior sensing and navigation through the boundary.

## Existing anchors

- The Rodal synthesis provides invariant curvature and stress-energy diagnostics for explicit warp geometries.
- Costa and Herdeiro provide a reminder that gravitational observables should be expressed covariantly rather than through loose EM analogy.
- Standard warp no-go and energy-condition results define constraints on known-matter implementations.

## What it does not yet provide

No current source identifies a buildable actuator, sourceable stress-energy distribution, or experimentally demonstrated worldtube.

The 2026 inverse design now quantifies the first source gate. For a `1 g`, `10 m` cavity with a `0.10 m` transition, a flat-spatial-slice target requires anisotropic stress of order `5.25 × 10^27 Pa`; a quiet-exterior weak-field mass representation requires paired positive/negative effective source mass with an absolute inventory around `1.9 × 10^14 kg`. A phase-localized metric mass has a formal micro-eV penetration window but no known microscopic portal or complete external support channel.

## Predicted signature classes

- correlated timing, phase, or lensing effects around a boundary;
- structured gradients rather than ordinary exhaust momentum;
- unusual but model-specific radiation or polarization signatures;
- environmental flow changes concentrated near a boundary surface;
- low internal acceleration paired with large externally reconstructed acceleration.

## Fatal constraints

- no conservation-complete acceleration mechanism;
- uncontrollable tidal or horizon formation;
- source-energy density irreducibly beyond physical reach;
- a model whose effects vanish when evaluated with invariant quantities.

## Next question

Construct an explicit external-source–mediator–gate action that supplies opposite momentum and makes the worldtube response universal for the hull, occupants, payload, clocks, and light. Test its field-transmission prediction before assigning propulsion significance.
