# Who's Actually Leading Warp & Breakthrough-Propulsion Research

**Scope:** spacetime-metric warp research *plus* the broader breakthrough/advanced-propulsion ecosystem (inertia/field-propulsion labs, funders, experimental groups). Every entry is tiered by **real output** — peer-reviewed papers, run experiments, funded programs — versus press releases.

**Date:** June 2026. Activity window emphasized: 2021–2026. Method: five parallel landscape reviews (theory, dedicated orgs, funders, experimental labs, companies/defense) + verification of contested specifics.

**Tier key:** **T1** leading, sustained real output · **T2** active/credible, clear niche lead · **T3** peripheral/early/contested · **T4** speculative/advocacy/mostly-claims · **T5** hype/debunked.

---

## The single most important finding

"Warp drive research" is not led by the people with "warp" in their company name. It is led by **theorists who keep the field honest and experimentalists who kill false positives.** The 2021–2026 pattern is unmistakable: every splashy "positive-energy / physical / FTL" warp announcement has been walked back by a smaller circle of rigorous critics — and those critics, plus the precision-measurement labs, are the actual center of gravity. The genuinely productive *organized* warp effort (Applied Physics) does real, peer-reviewed work but is strictly **subluminal, energy-prohibitive, and oversold**. The loudest "propulsion companies" (IVO, Exodus) have produced no verified thrust at all.

So the honest leaderboard inverts the hype ranking.

## Leaderboard — who leads, by function

**Theory — the intellectual center**
1. **Lyon group** (Buchert, Vigneron, Barzegar, Frackowiak — CRAL / ENS de Lyon, France) — **T1.** The most active warp-theory group of 2024–2026: proves the no-go theorems *and* builds the cleanest dynamical 3+1 formalism.
2. **Wellington group** (Matt Visser, Jessica Santiago, Sebastian Schuster — Victoria Univ. of Wellington, NZ) — **T1.** The rigor backbone; their "generic warp drives violate the null energy condition" (PRD 2022) is the rule every constructor now has to beat.
3. **José Rodal** (independent, US) — **T2 (rising).** The most interesting *constructor* right now: an irrotational, predominantly-positive-energy warp metric (GRG 2026) whose exoticity is the mildest yet — and which survived independent verification.

**Organized warp R&D**
4. **Applied Physics / Advanced Propulsion Laboratory** (Bobrick, Martire, Fuchs, Helmerich, Sellers) — **T2.** The only organization with a multi-year run of peer-reviewed warp papers and a reusable open-source tool (Warp Factory). Real output; subluminal and self-promotional.

**Experiment**
5. **Martin Tajmar / TU Dresden** — **T1.** The field's gold-standard tester; his null results (EmDrive, Mach-effect) are the most trusted experimental output in the entire space.
6. **Precision-gravity labs — Ulbricht (Southampton) & Aspelmeyer (Vienna)** — **T1 for decisive-test capability.** Not propulsion groups, but the only instruments sensitive enough (attonewton-scale) to actually confirm or kill small-scale inertia/gravity claims.

**Funding**
7. **NASA NIAC** — **T1 funder.** The only body consistently funding both exotic and credible concepts under technical review.
8. **Limitless Space Institute** — **T2 funder.** The leading *private* funder built for this, though weighed down by thin public output and a controversial R&D head.

Everything else is peripheral, advocacy, or hype.

## Profiles

### A. Theory — constructors and critics (universities + independents)

**Lyon group — CRAL, École Normale Supérieure de Lyon (France).** *University; both critic and constructor — T1.* Thomas Buchert (serious inhomogeneous-cosmology figure) with Quentin Vigneron, Hamed Barzegar, Antony Frackowiak. The most active warp-theory program going: Barzegar & Buchert diagnosed the hidden crutches shared by all existing warp metrics (Universe 2025); Barzegar–Buchert–Vigneron proved **several new no-go theorems** ([arXiv:2602.16495](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16495), Feb 2026); Buchert & Frackowiak built the constructive dynamical 3+1 counterpart ([arXiv:2605.03653](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03653), May 2026). If one group "leads warp theory" today, it's this one.

**Wellington group — Victoria University of Wellington (New Zealand).** *University; critics/verifiers — T1.* Matt Visser (a top authority on energy conditions), Jessica Santiago, Sebastian Schuster (now partly Charles Univ. Prague). ["Generic warp drives violate the null energy condition"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.03079) (PRD 105:064038, 2022) is the canonical refutation of the 2021 positive-energy claims. They construct almost nothing — that *is* their role, and it's why they're trusted.

**José Rodal — independent researcher (US; MIT Aero/Astro PhD).** *Individual constructor — T2, rising.* ["A warp drive with predominantly positive invariant energy density and global Hawking–Ellis Type I"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18008) (Gen. Rel. Grav. 2026) is the current "least-exotic" explicit warp solution — peak energy-condition violation ~60× milder than Natário — and notably survives the independent `warpax` check. Solo and unfunded, hence T2 not T1, but producing the most defensible new constructive physics.

**Alexey Bobrick — PhD Lund; Chief Science Officer, Applied Physics.** *Individual theorist embedded in an org — T2.* The credible academic anchor of the "physical warp drive" program ([Bobrick & Martire 2021, CQG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.06824); 60+ citations). Real contributor; the surrounding organization is where the hype lives (see B).

**Lavinia Heisenberg & Shaun Fell — Heidelberg / formerly ETH Zürich.** *University theorists — T3.* One of the original 2021 positive-energy claims ([arXiv:2104.06488](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.06488)), caught by the Wellington NEC theorem; no sustained warp-specific program since. Heisenberg is a strong modified-gravity figure otherwise.

**Erik Lentz — Pacific Northwest National Lab (US); formerly Göttingen.** *Individual — T3 (claim debunked).* The 2021 "hyperfast positive-energy soliton" was refuted by Wellington (2022) and again by Celmaster & Rubin (Nov 2025). His day job is axion/dark-matter detection; the warp headline outran the standing result.

**An T. Le — VinUniversity (Hanoi) / TU Darmstadt.** *Tool-builder/verifier — T3, watch.* Author of [`warpax`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.18023) (Feb 2026), a frame-independent energy-condition checker that quantifies the no-go result. Methodologically real, but a solo, brand-new repo by an ML/robotics researcher rather than an established GR authority.

**Miguel Alcubierre — ICN, UNAM (Mexico).** *Originator — T1 historically / T3 by current activity.* The 1994 metric founds the field, and he co-authored a 2025 review, but his active research is boson/Proca stars and numerical relativity, not new warp solutions. Maximum reputation, minimal current warp output by design. José Natário (IST Lisbon) is in the same position — foundational (the zero-expansion drive), no new warp papers in the window.

### B. Dedicated warp/propulsion organizations

**Applied Physics (PBC) — New York, USA (+ "Advanced Propulsion Laboratory AB," Stockholm).** *For-profit public-benefit corporation — T2; the clear niche leader.* Founder/CEO **Gianni Martire** (finance background, not a physicist); CSO Alexey Bobrick; collaborators Jared Fuchs & Christopher Helmerich (UAH PhD alumni), Luke Sellers (UCLA PhD student); Avi Loeb listed as an advisor. Output is genuine: [Introducing Physical Warp Drives (2021, CQG)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.06824); [Constant Velocity Physical Warp Drive (2024, CQG)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.02709); and the [**Warp Factory** open-source toolkit](https://newatlas.com/physics/warp-factory-software-warp-drive/) (2024) — their most distinctive, genuinely useful contribution. Revenue model: outsourced R&D, "VC due-diligence" services, and "$500k Phase I" Warp Grants (a PR-and-pipeline engine). **Critical reception is the key tension:** relativists call the 2021 work "legitimate but oversold" (Hossenfelder); the Lyon 2026 paper says their classification "is at best vague, suffers from some errors." Everything they produce is subluminal and energy-prohibitive. Leader of the dedicated-org niche by *productivity*, not by building anything close to a drive.

**Casimir (startup) — Harold "Sonny" White, founder/CEO (US).** *Deep-tech startup — T3–4.* Ex-NASA Eagleworks; also Director of Advanced R&D at Limitless Space Institute. Casimir's core is Casimir-effect nanotech power; its warp angle is the [2021 EPJC "Casimir geometry intersects the Alcubierre metric"](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1140/epjc/s10052-021-09484-z) result and a newer "warp nacelles" geometry. White famously called the 2021 result "a real, albeit humble and tiny, warp bubble" — a *numerical* Casimir result widely judged overhyped (Ethan Siegel, [Big Think](https://bigthink.com/starts-with-a-bang/no-warp-bubble/)). High visibility, low experimental yield. (A smaller entrant, "Astrum Drive," surfaces in 2025 SpaceCom promotion with a warp-bubble-geometry paper, but I couldn't substantiate it as a serious player — treat as unverified.)

### C. Funders & enablers

**NASA Innovative Advanced Concepts (NIAC) — US government.** *Funder — T1.* The de facto gatekeeper that turns fringe ideas into reviewed studies. Phase I ~$175k, Phase II ~$600k, Phase III up to $2M. Funded the Woodward/Fearn Mach-effect thruster (2017), and in-window work like Davoyan's pellet-beam propulsion (UCLA) and Helicity Space's fusion drive (2025). The only consistently real money for genuinely exotic concepts.

**Limitless Space Institute (LSI) — Houston, USA.** *Private nonprofit funder + education — T2.* Founded and chaired by **Dr. Kam Ghaffarian** (founder of SGT, Axiom Space, Intuitive Machines, X-Energy); president **Brian "BK" Kelly** (37 years at NASA, ex-Director of Flight Operations); **Sonny White** as Director of Advanced R&D. Funds university advanced-propulsion work via Interstellar Initiative grants (tactical ≤$100k, strategic ≤$250k). The leading *private* funder explicitly chartered for this — caveats: thin public output and a contested R&D head.

**DARPA — US government.** *Funder — T1 capacity / T3 for breakthrough physics.* Underwrote the ~$1.3M quantized-inertia grant to McCulloch (2018–2022), now ended; current programs target conventional advanced maneuvering, not exotic physics. Willing to touch the fringe, quick to exit.

**Tau Zero Foundation — US nonprofit.** *Advocacy/lineage — T3.* Marc Millis (ex-lead of NASA's Breakthrough Propulsion Physics). The reference text *Frontiers of Propulsion Science* (2009) and a 2017 NASA interstellar-propulsion review; low current throughput. Credibility broker, not a funder.

**Breakthrough Starshot — privately funded (Milner/Hawking, 2016).** *Program — T2 historically / T4 now.* The best-funded interstellar-propulsion effort ever ($100M pledged for laser light-sails) — but only ~$4.5–5M was ever disbursed, and it's in a "[quiet demise](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/the-quiet-demise-of-breakthrough-starshot-a-billionaires-interstellar/)." A cautionary tale of pledge-versus-spend. (i4is, the Interstellar Research Group, and Icarus Interstellar round out the lane as conference/advocacy bodies — T4, with Icarus effectively dormant.)

### D. Experimental labs

**Martin Tajmar — TU Dresden, Chair of Space Systems (Germany).** *University aerospace lab — T1.* The "SpaceDrive" / Breakthrough Propulsion Physics program. The [2021 EmDrive null](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/350108418) ruled out earlier thrust claims by 2–3 orders of magnitude; same null for Mach-effect thrusters; a [2024 *Scientific Reports* search](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-70286-w) found no EM–gravity coupling. He leads the experimental side precisely *because* his null results are the most decisive output the field produces — the benchmark every claimant must clear.

**Precision-gravity / levitated-mass labs — Hendrik Ulbricht (Southampton, UK) & Markus Aspelmeyer (Vienna).** *University physics labs — T1 for decisive-test capability.* Ulbricht's group (lead author Tim Fuchs) [measured gravity from a 0.43 mg source mass at ~30 attonewtons](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.adk2949) (Science Advances 2024); Aspelmeyer pioneers gram-scale gravity and entanglement tests. Neither chases warp drives — but their sensitivity is years ahead of anything the propulsion claimants use, making them the only groups who could *decisively* test small-scale inertia/gravity-modification claims.

**Heidi Fearn / Space Studies Institute — CSU Fullerton Woodward lab (US).** *Institute-funded lab — T3.* Continues the Mach-effect thruster program after James Woodward's death (Aug 2025). Active, but the central thrust claim remains unreplicated by independent precision labs (Tajmar's null stands).

**Mike McCulloch — University of Plymouth (UK).** *University lab — T3.* Quantized-inertia theory and experiments; ex-DARPA funding. The 2025 IVO orbital data was inconclusive; most relevant, he's in 2026 talks with the UK's National Physical Laboratory for a metrology-grade ground test — which would be the first rigorous external check of QI, but hasn't run yet.

### E. Companies & defense-adjacent (the hype lane)

**IVO Ltd — "Quantum Drive" (US).** *Company — T5.* Richard Mansell; thruster premised on McCulloch's QI. The Barry-1 satellite test (2023) [died before the drive ever fired](https://thedebrief.org/breaking-satellite-failure-scuttles-first-of-its-kind-in-space-test-of-physics-defying-quantum-drive/); a 2025 follow-up produced only an inconclusive, confounded orbital-decay difference — no orbit-raising. Loudest, emptiest.

**Exodus Propulsion Technologies — Charles Buhler (US).** *Company — T4–5.* Asymmetric-electrostatic "1 g, propellantless" claim (APEC 2023), reportedly persisting after power-off (an energy-conservation red flag). Zero peer-reviewed papers, zero independent replication, no space test. Credentialed lead, classic artifact signature.

**Hal Puthoff / EarthTech International (US); Eric Davis; Salvatore Pais.** *Defense-adjacent theory/advocacy/patents — T4–T5.* Puthoff is the intellectual architect of the "metric engineering" framing (AAWSAP reference documents); Davis authored speculative warp/wormhole surveys and testified at the 2025 House UAP hearing; Pais (now US Space Force) holds the "inertial mass reduction" Navy patents that NAWCAD spent $508k on and concluded "could not be demonstrated." Theory, patents, and UAP politics — no devices.

## Honest takeaways

**Who to actually watch.** For warp *theory*, watch **Lyon** (where the live results are) and **Rodal** (the most defensible new construction); **Wellington** sets the bar. For the question of whether any inertia/gravity effect is real, watch the **experimentalists** — **Tajmar** to keep everyone honest, and **Southampton/Vienna** because they're the ones who could actually detect a tiny real effect. For money, watch **NIAC** and **LSI**.

**The constructor-vs-critic split is the story.** The exciting headlines come from constructors (Applied Physics, Lentz, White); the durable results come from critics and testers. Over 2021–2026 the critics have won every round — which is itself the honest state of the art: warp research is maturing as a *theoretical* discipline (better formalisms, cleaner no-go theorems, milder-exoticity solutions) while remaining nowhere near an actuator or a verified laboratory effect.

**Geography.** Leadership is concentrated in the US, Western Europe, and New Zealand. The one-time non-Western contender — China's NWPU EmDrive work — was retracted years ago; there is no publicly known leading Chinese or Russian warp program.

**The blunt version.** The most visible "warp/propulsion" brands (IVO, Exodus, the Pais patents, and to a lesser extent White's "warp bubble") are the emptiest on verified output. The quiet theory groups and the rigorous null-result labs are the ones genuinely leading the charge — and what they're leading is a careful, sobering clarification of how hard this is, not a countdown to a drive.
