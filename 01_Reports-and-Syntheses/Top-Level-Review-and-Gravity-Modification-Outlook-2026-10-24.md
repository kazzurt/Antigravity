# Top-Level Review of the Antigravity Program and a Gravity-Modification Outlook

**Date:** 2026-10-24
**Scope:** (I) a top-level review of this research effort as it stands in the repository; (II) the state of the publicly available literature from roughly April through October 2026, with emphasis on what changed after the program's June 2026 landscape reports and its 2026-09-04 handoff; (III) a big-picture outlook on where gravity modification, if it is possible at all, is most likely to come from, as scenarios with probability ranges and named actors.
**Method:** two orchestrated agent workflows. The first ran six independent readers over the repository's lanes and ten literature scouts over distinct search angles, then a completeness critic and eight targeted follow-up sweeps (25 agents, about 1,700 tool calls). The second ran two independent fact-checkers (an existence lens and an overselling lens) on each of 22 load-bearing literature claims, three independent program critics (referee, strategist, epistemics auditor), and a five-persona forecasting panel with a synthesis step. The reviewing agent also read the control-layer documents, the latest reports, and several primary pages directly (arXiv abstracts, the PURSUE portal, AARO coverage). Claims below are labeled where they rest on an agent's fetch rather than the author's own read.
**Status vocabulary:** this document follows the knowledgebase convention. "Closed" means closed within a stated model; "conditional" means a result that depends on an unrealized input; nothing here is an experimental result.
**What this document does not do:** it does not modify any existing knowledgebase file, the log, the index, or the evidence register. Corrections it recommends are listed in §I.6 for the owner to apply.

## Bottom line

1. **The program is epistemically honest and strategically stalled.** It has produced seven completed theory gates since July 2026 and all seven returned a no-go or an unrealized conditional. Each closure has been followed by promotion of a less-specified architecture (Li–Torr → Tate collective modes → phase-gated scalar → metric-Higgs shell → bimetric transformer → externally supported field-sail). The current rank-1 lane is a requirements statement with no mediator, action, material, or number attached. There is no program-level kill criterion, no experiment, no collaborator, no external reader, no publication, and the last log entry is seven weeks old. Version control holds two commits from one 28-minute window on 2026-07-24, one never pushed; all September work is uncommitted.
2. **The physics the program has done is mostly sound and some of it is genuinely useful.** The separation of cavity field cancellation from whole-craft support, the rank-one London–GEM theorem, the Li–Torr angular-momentum refutation, and the 5.25 × 10^27 Pa / 1.9 × 10^14 kg worldtube benchmark are correct within their models and dispose of a large fraction of the historical "antigravity" literature. The weaknesses are scope inflation in the prose, a straw-man flavor in one headline number, incomplete back-propagation of the September result into the July documents, and a handful of bibliographic errors that matter because the program's credibility rests on citation hygiene.
3. **The public record from April to October 2026 contains no evidence-grade gravity or inertia anomaly, and the trend is toward tighter constraints, better instruments, and institutional maturation of the UAP question without any physics content.** The items that should change the program's reading list are: An T. Le's two shell papers and the Bolívar–Abellán–Vasilev hollow-core and Natário-minimum papers (they work the program's own shell-support problem from the outside and reach consistent conclusions); Modrekiladze's effective field theory of gravity in relativistic media (the yardstick for any "gravitational permeability" claim: medium effects are G-suppressed with no amplification); the LARES-2 frame-dragging measurement (0.2 %); the superfluid-helium gyrometer proposal (a tabletop frame-dragging sensor on paper); and the fact that cavity-vacuum control of superconductivity is now a reproducible 5–13 % knob across four groups, which is exactly the kind of material gate the program wanted and which still says nothing about gravity.
4. **On the outlook question, the honest answer is that if gravity modification arrives in a living person's lifetime it will almost certainly arrive as a small, unexpected anomaly in a precision instrument that was built for something else, followed by decades of engineering, rather than as a propulsion device from a startup or a disclosure from a government.** Section III gives the scenarios, ranges, and names.

## Part I — Review of the research effort

### I.1 What the program is

A requirements-first theory program, maintained by one person and AI agents, that adopts as a fixed search directive (P0/P1 in the assumption ladder) that an engineered frame-decoupling craft exists and that some UAP reports observe it, and then inverse-designs a **protected worldtube**: a bounded region whose occupants stay near-geodesic and low-tidal while the complete source–shell–payload system changes trajectory. The reference target fixed in July is 1 g over a 10 m cabin with a 1 % interior tidal tolerance, which corresponds to a metric amplitude of only gL/c² ≈ 1.09 × 10^-15. The difficulty the program correctly identified is not producing that amplitude but terminating and supporting the gradient at a finite boundary and naming what receives the opposite four-momentum.

The current rank-1 lane, the **externally supported universal worldtube / field-sail hybrid**, is in plain language a sail: because a closed craft cannot push itself, some external field (Earth-linked, beamed, ambient, or radiated) must supply direction and reaction momentum, and an onboard sector must convert that pull into a change of the single physical metric so that hull, occupants, clocks, and light accelerate together. No mediator, action, or material for any part of this exists. The lane ranks first because it is the only one that addresses energy, direction, reservoir, and protected interior at once, which is also why it is the least constrained.

### I.3 Strengths

- The two-mode posture (discovery and audit) is written down, and the anti-contamination rule separating premise status from evidence status is honored in the source ratings.
- No-go results are quantitative, scoped, and candid; the program kills its own proposals with its own numbers.
- Invariant discipline: every derivation targets the physical lapse gradient, proper acceleration, and Weyl components rather than coordinate GEM fields. This alone disposes of most Li–Torr and Podkletnov-style literature.
- The distinction between interior cancellation and whole-system support, with an explicit recoil ledger, is a real conceptual contribution that most of the field misses.
- Calculations ship as reproducible Python with assertion suites; headline numbers recomputed by an independent reader reproduce to quoted precision.
- Evidence register pairs each source with a "supported use" and a "do not treat as established" column; the relationship map's "important non-links" list is an effective guard against analogy creep.
- Falsifications of alternative theories are stated with numbers (Stepanović's 5/6 perihelion coefficient; Monjo's Δα/α against white-dwarf limits) and graded honestly.

### I.4 Weaknesses

**Structural**

- Degenerating-programme pattern: seven gates, seven negatives, each followed by a more abstract hypothesis. The documents do not confront this. The July review's own §6.5 said a program whose success case sits twenty orders from its destination "arguably should redirect"; the redirect went toward bimetric gravity, relativistic supersolids, and Floquet converters rather than toward anything buildable.
- No program-level kill criterion. The change rule in the current-path document lists only positive re-rank triggers; the mission's success criteria are all process outputs. The adopted premise is insulated from the stop-condition culture that governs everything beneath it.
- The premise is quarantined from source ratings but not from problem definition. The capability model is the UAP report taxonomy; P0's "plausible for a residual subset" is asserted without any case in the register meeting the program's own admission rule. The 1 g / 10 m target and the FTL "seventh layer" are design targets, not derived ones.
- The declared eight-criterion thread scorecard is never applied; three overlapping taxonomies (7 mechanism families, 5 ranked lanes, 5 threads) have no crosswalk; the rank-1 lane carries no status label.

**Allocation**

- The Tate thread has absorbed three long reports, two codebases, and a source manifest on a 36-year-old single-laboratory residual (92 ± 21 ppm against a theory comparator with no stated uncertainty) whose gravitational relevance every document disclaims and whose only follow-up (Hoang 2020) disagrees by a factor of five. By the program's own rank-one identifiability result, further theory cannot progress; the cheap blocking action (obtain the Tate 1990 PRB and Hoang 2020 full texts) has been "blocking" since 2026-07-16.
- The gravity-response block η_g is defined in three documents and never evaluated, even as a baseline. The lane has never written down what a confirmed 9.2 × 10^-5 neutral mode would do gravitationally; the answer is ordinary GR at the 10^-30 s^-1 scale.
- The program is entirely desk-based; zero experiments have been run in five years of activity. The current best path's proposed experiments (nested normal/ordered shell with modulated source mass and dual gravimeters; twin-niobium phase reversal at 10^-12 m s^-2) require cryogenic precision-force laboratories the program does not have and has not costed.
- The proposed experiments have thresholds set by sensor reach, not by any model prediction, so a null would constrain an unspecified portal product and teach little about the craft question.

**Hygiene**

- Verification is asserted, not recorded. The paper-verification helper never functioned; its only artifact tracks nine Sarfatti papers no report cites. The Tate–London-moment report's 18-paper table carries UNVERIFIED on 17 rows.
- Internal review is agent-on-agent, and the documents' own disclaimers are the only acknowledgement. No human outside the owner has read any of this.
- Routing is fragile: at least six control documents and several "historical" banners must be read in order to learn what governs; the 2026-07-16 episode already showed two agents acting on divergent cards.

### I.6 Recommendations

Refined in §I.7 after the independent critique panel; the items below follow directly from the reading.

1. **Write the kill criterion.** State, in the mission document, what observation or theorem would demote the premise or stop the program. Candidates: no covariant source-complete action for the rank-1 lane by a fixed date; no identified reservoir for both hover and deep space; Gaia DR4 and the next torsion-balance generation closing the last low-acceleration and short-range windows.
2. **Stop relocating the source-scale gap.** Before any further lane is opened, the next theory product should be an estimate of what, even in principle, could supply 10^16 J m^-3 of switchable anisotropic stress, or a proof that nothing in known field theory can. If the latter, the program's destination is a premise about undiscovered physics, and it should say so.
3. **Close or finish the Tate thread by purchase, not derivation.** Obtain Tate 1990 (PRB 42, 7885) and Hoang 2020 with corrigendum through a library; if Hoang does not report a niobium residual, strike the "18 ppm" row and demote the thread to a matter-metrology footnote. No further algebra is warranted by the program's own identifiability result.
4. **Tie any experiment to a predicted signal.** Propose no measurement without a model-predicted magnitude and a field-and-artifact budget with matched controls (sample-absent, RF-off, detuned, spin-zero, sign-reversal).
5. **Get one human physicist to read one document.** The London–GEM eigenmode theorem and the worldtube source/stress benchmark are the two results most likely to survive a referee; post one as a short note (arXiv endorsement will be needed) or send it to someone already working the shell-support problem from the outside (An T. Le and the Bolívar group are the obvious addresses; see §II).
6. **Fix version control today.** Commit and push the September work; adopt a rule that no log entry exists until it is committed.
7. **Install a citation protocol.** Every post-2024 citation written by an agent gets a Crossref or arXiv API resolution recorded in a machine-readable file before it enters a report; the eleven corrections above are what happens otherwise.

## Part II — The public literature, April–October 2026

### II.1 Headline

Eighteen search lanes, about 420 distinct items, 270 of them new since the program's June 2026 snapshot. No lane produced an independently replicated laboratory gravity or inertia anomaly, no new propellantless-thrust result passed peer review, no national metrology laboratory tested any claim, and no funder anywhere (NSF, ERC/EIC, UKRI, ESA, JAXA, NASA NIAC, DARPA) paid for gravity or inertia modification in 2026. What moved: constraints tightened, instruments crossed new floors, warp theory sharpened its no-go results while gaining one honest positive-energy construction that turns out to be a rocket, cavity-controlled superconductivity became a reproducible material knob, and the United States built a legal and institutional pipeline for UAP disclosure that has so far carried no physics. The one structural novelty bearing on the lifetime-horizon question is not physical: AI systems began mass-producing and mass-refereeing frontier manuscripts, and arXiv throttled submissions in response on 2026-10-01.

### II.2 Warp and metric engineering

- **An T. Le (VinUniversity, Hanoi; TU Darmstadt; a robotics professor writing GR "in my free time")** is the most active constructive author of 2026 and the person working the program's own problem. *Relativistic elastic shells: material support and cavity geometry* (arXiv:2605.25417, v3 2026-09-17) shows that a static spherical shell with two free vacuum faces cannot be supported by nonnegative isotropic pressure alone but can be by tangential elastic stress satisfying the dominant energy condition in weak gravity, and derives the cavity tides and clock shifts. *Radiative steering of warp shells* (arXiv:2606.22531, v1 2026-06-21 titled "Steering a warp drive without exotic matter", v4 2026-09-13 "substantially revised and retitled") matches a flat cavity to a Kinnersley photon-rocket exterior: the shell steers subluminally with nonnegative emitted radiation under a fuel bound m_f/m_i = e^{-3L}, self-similar shells require anisotropic stress and have growing normal modes, and post-turn self-gravitating settling is open. In plain terms it is a photon rocket carrying a cavity, which is the opposite architecture from the program's externally supported sail, but it is the first explicit positive-energy treatment of the acceleration phase and it is exactly where the program's "deep space cannot reuse Earth as reservoir" result points. His `warpax` toolkit (nine releases May–October, v1.6.0 on 2026-10-22; arXiv:2602.18023 now at v6) certifies that every bubble-wall geometry tested, including Rodal's, violates the null energy condition for some observer. Reviewed so far only by an AI referee (pith.science), which flagged an unproven identity behind the time-evolved claim. *(Author read the arXiv abstracts; affiliation and version history confirmed by the follow-up sweep.)*
- **Bolívar, Abellán, Vasilev (Universidad Central de Venezuela; also listed with Astrum Drive Technologies, a Texas thruster startup)** posted two results that bear directly on the program's worldtube inverse design: *Boundary obstructions and lapse freedom in static spherical hollow cores* (arXiv:2608.15000, August) shows that in the unit-lapse flat-slice class any regular nonnegative density rising out of a flat cavity must violate the transverse null and weak energy conditions, and that releasing the lapse admits regular hollow shells; and *Zero expansion is not free* (arXiv:2609.36211, 2026-09-28) proves the Natário thin-wall negative-energy minimum scales as v²R⁴/(4Δ³), two powers of R/Δ worse than the old estimate, with the variational problem equivalent to Stokes flow between concentric spheres. *(Natário paper read by the author; hollow-core paper from the sweep, verification pending in the appendix.)*

Nothing was found on an externally supported or field-sail bubble.

LAGEOS data also pushed the preferred-frame parameter α₁ to about 2 × 10^-5. *(Verification pending.)*

It is a design, not a measurement; the gating millikelvin junctions are being developed in **Keith Schwab's Caltech lab**. If built, it would make any Tajmar-type rotating-superconductor gravitomagnetic claim testable at many orders better sensitivity. *(Verification pending.)*
- **Tajmar (TU Dresden)** published another null in July 2026 (Int. J. Mod. Phys. A 41, 2650147: no anomalous force from toroidal solenoids predicted by modified Kaluza–Klein theory, to about 0.1 mg), and his chair's Breakthrough Propulsion Physics page (updated January 2026) states the 2006-era frame-dragging signals vanished with better isolation. ### II.4 Precision gravity and tabletop quantum gravity

- Levitated-mass platforms matured fast: **Leiden/Rice** (Oosterkamp, Uitenbroek, Tunnell, Amaral, Qin) reached 0.07 fN/√Hz and picometer, sub-10 mK control of a milligram magnet and ran the first ultraheavy-dark-matter search with a levitated particle (POLONAISE); **Southampton** (Ulbricht, Fuchs) resolved Earth tides with an 18 μGal diamagnetically levitated gravimeter and, with **Budker**, proposed a levitated-magnet vector sensor for spin-dependent forces; **USTC/Nanjing** (Huang, Yin, Kong) improved spin-spin-velocity exotic-force limits by 12–13 orders at centimeter scales; **ISTA** (Hosten) laser-cooled a 1 mg torsional pendulum to 240 μK (Commun. Phys. 9, 80). All first physics limits are on dark matter and exotic spin forces, not on gravity itself; the Yukawa fifth-force search (MORRIS) is still a proposal.
- Atom interferometry hit infrastructure milestones: **AION** (Nature 654, 622, June) validated noise-immune differential Sr clock-transition interferometry; **MAGIS-100** completed its laser lab with commissioning around 2028; **CERN's AICE** technical proposal (100 m, 57 institutions); and the **China Space Station** dual-rubidium WEP test (Zhan group, Science Advances, August) reached η = (−2.7 ± 4.7) × 10^-7 over 280 days, historic for quantum matter in orbit and six orders weaker than torsion balances.
- **Eöt-Wash** holds the floors: superconducting Nb versus Cu η ≤ 2.0 × 10^-9 and Cooper pairs ≤ 9.2 × 10^-4 (PRD 2025); Be–Al toward the Sun η ≤ 2.1 × 10^-13 (arXiv:2602.02815, February 2026, preprint); its 2025–2026 output has shifted to dark-matter torsion-balance searches. **HUST** LLR reanalysis sits at about 3 × 10^-14. **NIST/Birmingham** replicated the BIPM G measurement at 5.7 × 10^-5, pulling the field toward consensus.
