## 1. Executive judgment

The knowledgebase is in unusually good epistemic condition: the three 2026 theory reports are faithful to the primary sources (all sixteen audited claims verified against the Li/Torr PDFs), the arithmetic checks out (I re-derived the 21–24 order gap, the `4×10^-32 C/kg` conventional cross-coefficient, and the `1.1×10^-9` niobium-inertia parameterization independently), and the reframing of Li's mechanism into a matter-response question times a gravity-response question is the correct factorization. This review therefore does not overturn the project's direction. It sharpens it, adds constraints the knowledgebase missed, and downgrades one piece of evidence.

The five headline judgments:

1. **Li's amplification is not merely unproven; it is multiply self-undermining.** Beyond the known breaks (ion phase assignment, forced winding, permeability denominator), re-inspection of the primary papers shows that the 1993 equilibrium value of `μ_r` is selected through the requirement that the superconductor be a "force free region" (its Eq. 2.27) — the small denominator that drives the gain is not an independent measurement. The 1992 model also predicts a coupled condensate/field phase velocity `c/√μ_g,r ≈ 10^6`–`3×10^6 m/s` (its Eq. 30). That prediction is worth testing against mode spectroscopy, but non-observation is not a falsifier until the predicted mode's coupling and the relevant experimental coverage are derived. Repair of the published amplification is not a live option; only replacement is.
2. **Angular-momentum bookkeeping is a promising additional constraint on Li's static counterflow, but its numerical strength remains provisional.** Historical gyromagnetic and London-moment experiments on superconductors (Kikoin–Gubar 1940; Pry, Lathrop, Houston 1952; Doll 1958; Hildebrandt 1964) are consistent with the free-electron angular-momentum/magnetic-moment relation. Li's flux-linked ionic current would carry far more angular momentum per canceled charge current. The claimed percent-level accuracy and resulting six-order exclusion must be re-derived from the original apparatus data before being entered as a binding quantitative bound.
3. **The Tate residual is a genuine quantitative anomaly, but it is weaker than the knowledgebase previously treated it.** It is a single unrepeated measurement from 1989–1990, and the theoretical baseline carries an internal spread of order 8 ppm (Cabrera–Peskin vs. Jiang–Liu vs. Lipavský). Hoang 2020 cannot presently serve as independent corroboration because its full article, referenced supplementary apparatus material, raw data, and corrigendum have not all been obtained and audited. A rotating-superconductor response-matrix calculation remains valuable, but targeted negative searches establish an open search gap rather than guaranteed novelty.
4. **The neutral-eigenmode object is sound after one structural correction: an ordinary lattice must enter as an elastic displacement field, not as a second phase field.** Writing the ion system as `θ_ion` presupposes ionic coherence — the exact error that broke Li. A London-coefficient protection theorem is a useful conventional matter-sector baseline and a potential standalone side project. It is not the current top-level mechanism milestone, because even a real unconventional London response would still leave the source-to-gravity law missing.
5. **Established superconductor–gravity amplification is closed; the broader gravity-side theory search is not.** Published enhanced-response claims in this domain have been refuted or tightly bounded, while conventional derived susceptibilities remain ordinary stress-energy responses. The current lead therefore asks a different question: whether an explicit, covariant, screened or phase-gated universal field has a nonempty allowed parameter window. Superconducting response enters that program as a gate baseline and discriminator, not as presumed gravitational gain.

**Bottom line:** the superconducting response-matrix thread is retained as a high-value conventional baseline and possible companion calculation, not as rank-1 mechanism selection. The active rank-1 milestone is the phase-gated universal-field downselection because it attacks the missing source-to-field law directly. Nothing in the present superconducting matter-sector physics connects to the craft requirement without a separate new interaction and an enormous scale bridge.

## 4. The central technical object, examined

The knowledgebase proposes as the next theoretical object:

> A neutral eigenmode of a multicomponent coherent medium whose electromagnetic current is screened or canceled while its mass, angular momentum, or full stress-energy response remains nonzero,

with schematic

\[
\mathbf u_a=\nabla\theta_a-\frac{q_a}{\hbar}\mathbf A-\frac{m_a}{\hbar}\mathbf A_g,
\qquad
F_{\rm kin}=\frac12\sum_{ab}\rho_{ab}\,\mathbf u_a\cdot\mathbf u_b,
\qquad
q^T\rho\,e_N\approx0,\quad m^T\rho\,e_N\neq0.
\]

Requested checks, performed:

**Dimensions and normalization.** Consistent if `A_g` carries velocity-like units (specific momentum) in the chosen GEM convention: each term is an inverse length, so `u_a = (m_a/ℏ)v_a` and `ρ_ab` is a phase stiffness (units J/m), not a mass density. Two housekeeping requirements for the real calculation: (i) fix the GEM convention (harmonic-gauge `h_{0i}`, factor conventions per Mashhoon/Malekolkalami–Farhoudi) once, at the top; (ii) state the map between `ρ_ab` and the Andreev–Bashkin mass-density matrix `ρ^{AB}_{ab} = (m_a m_b/ℏ^2)^{-1}…` explicitly, because the Galilean constraints below are standard in the AB normalization.

**Gauge structure.** Electromagnetic: `A → A + ∇χ`, `θ_a → θ_a + (q_a/ℏ)χ` leaves every `u_a` invariant — sound, provided *every* coherent component transforms with its own charge; a component with `q_a = 0` must not shift. Gravitational: `A_g` is not gauge-invariant under weak-field diffeomorphisms; `h_{0i}` shifts inhomogeneously under `x^i → x^i + ξ^i(t)`, which is compensated only if each `θ_a` absorbs the corresponding `m_a`-weighted phase (the nonrelativistic residue of `exp(im c^2 τ/ℏ)` proper-time phases). This is exactly the point Inan 2022 makes about coordinate-dependent gravitomagnetic fields. Consequence for the calculation: only *differences* against the co-rotating lattice frame and closed-loop (fluxoid) integrals are observables; any intermediate quantity linear in `A_g` alone is convention. The schematic passes this test only if used through such invariants.

**Rotating-frame treatment.** The physically correct formulation is: work in the frame co-rotating with the lattice at `Ω`, where equilibrium is defined; the rotation enters through the lattice velocity field `v_L = Ω×r` (plus elastic corrections `\dot u`), the centrifugal/electrochemical potentials, and — if gravity is kept — the frame-dragging piece of `A_g`. The knowledgebase's component list already includes "rotation or frame vorticity" as an entry; the review's correction is that rotation is not another *field* on the same footing but a *boundary/frame condition* imposed through the lattice. Treating it as a field invites double counting against `A_g` (both enter as `v`-like potentials).

**Relativistic limitations.** The quadratic Galilean form is adequate for `Ω r/c ~ 10^-8` laboratory physics; the known relativistic corrections to the London coefficient (`~10^-8`, Cabrera–Gutfreund–Little/Brady) enter through the electrochemical sector, not through `F_kin` — the model must therefore include the electrostatic potential and band/interaction energies or it cannot even reproduce the ±8 ppm baseline dispute it is meant to adjudicate.

**Double counting.** Two hazards. (i) The lattice appears both as a coherent "component" and as the frame — it must appear exactly once, as an elastic medium with displacement field `u(r,t)`, whose uniform rigid rotation defines the frame and whose fluctuations are phonons. (ii) Electromagnetic screening appears both in `u_a` (via `A`) and in any constitutive `μ` — use vacuum constants plus explicit currents only (the audit's C4 rule), or the Kowitt error re-enters through the back door.

**Stress-energy definition.** `j_m = −δF/δA_g` is a legitimate *definition* of the mass-current response only at the Galilean level. The full object is `T^{μν}` from the covariant action; at minimum the calculation must verify `∂_μ T^{μ0} = 0` and that the momentum density equals the mass current (Galilean Ward identity), which is exactly where a fake "electrically quiet, mechanically active" mode would be caught: a mode can only carry momentum that is physically present in its constituents.

**Lattice degrees of freedom — the structural correction.** As written, the schematic assigns each component a compact phase `θ_a`. A crystalline lattice has no such phase; its Goldstone fields are displacements, and a "lattice phase" with nonzero winding is precisely Li's Eq. 22 error in modern dress. A solid can only join the phase-mode algebra if it hosts defect condensation (supersolidity) or a genuine second condensate — neither exists in any superconductor. **The response matrix must therefore be built on fields `{θ (condensate), u (elastic lattice), A, φ_e}` plus at most one *hypothetical* second phase `χ` carried as an explicitly labeled counterfactual.** With that substitution the object is sound.

**Relation to established theory.** The corrected object reduces, term by term, to known physics: London/GL electrodynamics (θ, A), elasticity and phonons (u), electrochemical corrections (φ_e; Cabrera–Peskin sector), Andreev–Bashkin entrainment (θ–χ cross-stiffness), and Babaev–Ashcroft two-condensate response (θ, χ both active). Nothing in it is exotic; its value is that it makes the *absence* of a second phase-rigid component a derivable statement rather than an assumption.

**Verdict on the object:** **a sound organizing model after one correction** — replace the ionic phase field with an elastic displacement field and demote the second phase to an explicit hypothetical. It is not the wrong abstraction, and it is more than a heuristic: with the correction it is the standard-physics completion of Li's Eq. 8.4 kinematics.

**Is the proposed rotating-superconductor response matrix the best next calculation? It is a strong matter-sector calculation, but not the top-level project milestone.** The active phase-gated-gravity milestone must first test whether any explicit universal-field architecture survives constraints. Within the superconducting side project, the physics points to a sharper primary deliverable:

> **A protection theorem for the London coefficient.** In the co-rotating frame, bulk equilibrium minimizes the free energy at zero relative velocity between condensate and lattice regardless of the stiffness/entrainment values, forcing `B = −(2m/e)Ω` with the bare mass, up to (i) electrochemical/relativistic terms (the ±8 ppm sector), (ii) surface-layer physics within `λ(T)` (a measurement-layer correction, not a bulk one), (iii) nonequilibrium response, and (iv) terms present only if a second independently phase-rigid component exists (Babaev–Ashcroft violation of the London law). Entrainment between the condensate and a *normal* (clamped) subsystem renormalizes dynamics, not the equilibrium coefficient.

If this theorem survives derivation — and Cabrera–Peskin's cancellation of band renormalizations, M. Liu's frame-independent London equation (good to `10^-10`), and Jiang–Liu's thermodynamic closure all say it will — then the Tate residual cannot be a bulk equilibrium matter effect in standard niobium, and the live options collapse to: experimental systematics (λ(T) extrapolation, trapped flux, geometry), the disputed ±8 ppm electrochemical sector, nonequilibrium spin-up physics, or a second coherent component that niobium does not have. That is a *decision*, not a review. The inversion step (what `ρ_ab` structure would produce 92 or 18 ppm) then quantifies exactly how exotic any real-anomaly interpretation must be, and the theorem's loophole list becomes the discriminant table for any future repeat experiment.

The component list in the review prompt is endorsed with amendments: superconducting phase/current ✓; ionic displacement and rigid rotation ✓ (as elastic field + frame, per above); electromagnetic and electrochemical fields ✓ (mandatory, not optional); **one independently justified neutral collective field — amended:** in niobium there is no independently justified candidate, so carry one *hypothetical* `χ` with free `(ρ_χ, ρ_{θχ})` and let the data bound it; entrainment/cross-stiffness ✓; rotation as frame ✓; weak gravitational sources only after matter closure ✓ (and in version 1, drop `A_g` entirely — it contributes at the `10^-20` level and only obscures the ppm accounting).

## 5. Tate, Hoang, `μ_g`, and the candidate collective fields

### 5.1 What the Tate anomaly is, precisely, as of July 2026

Measured: `m_app/2m_e = 1.000084(21)` (PRL 62, 845 (1989); PRB 42, 7885 (1990)). Baseline: `0.999992` per Cabrera–Peskin (PRB 39, 6425 (1989)), building on the −8 ppm relativistic/electrostatic correction of Cabrera–Gutfreund–Little (PRB 25, 6644 (1982)) and Brady (JLTP 49, 1 (1982), `eB = 2(m_0 − W/c^2)ω`). Disputes about the baseline: Jiang–Liu (PRB 63, 184506 (2001)) find the correction cancels in a consistent thermodynamic treatment (baseline `1.000000`); Lipavský (Physica C 533, 161 (2017)) shows the correction is representation-dependent (BCS vs. BEC). Framework-level protections: band renormalizations cancel (Cabrera–Peskin); the London relation is thermodynamically exact to `~10^-10` (M. Liu, PRL 81, 3223 (1998) — note: this paper is a *frame-independent London equation* derivation, not a strain proposal; no strain/lattice-relaxation explanation of Tate exists anywhere in the literature). Hirsch (Phys. Scr. 89, 015806 (2014)) treats the appearance of the *bare* mass itself as a puzzle for BCS; Koizumi (JSNM 34, 1361 (2021)) offers a Berry-connection explanation of the bare mass — neither touches the +92 ppm. Nothing published between 2022 and July 2026 confronts the residual quantitatively (closest: Markoš–Hlubina, Physica C (2025/26), London-field build-up dynamics).

Status therefore: a single, never-repeated, ~4σ formal anomaly against a baseline with ~8 ppm internal theory spread, in an observable otherwise protected to `10^-10`. Both stronger and stranger than a generic unexplained residual: the protection theorems make a *conventional bulk* explanation nearly impossible, which is exactly why the systematics hypothesis (penetration-depth extrapolation to `T=0`, trapped flux in type-II niobium, geometry corrections) and the second-component hypothesis are the only serious survivors — and why no one has closed it in 36 years.

### 5.2 The gravitomagnetic interpretation is closed

For the record, with primary sources: explaining Tate gravitationally requires `B_g ≈ 1.84×10^-4 Ω` — roughly 30 orders above the GR field of the ring. Tajmar–de Matos proposed exactly this (Physica C 385, 551 (2003); gr-qc/0607086); the claimed laboratory signals were excluded independently (Graham, Hurst, Thirkettle, Rowe, Butler, Physica C 468, 383 (2008): ring laser, ≥21× smaller than predicted), fell 5 orders short of the Tate requirement even at face value (Tajmar et al., arXiv:0707.3806), tracked the liquid helium rather than the superconductor (AIP Conf. Proc. 1208, 220 (2010)), and were finally bounded at `(1.9±4)×10^-11` coupling — ~7 orders below requirement — with the earlier signals attributed to acoustic/vibration artifacts (Tajmar, SuST 24, 125011 (2011); reaffirmed in Tajmar–Neunzig–Kößling, Front. Phys. 10, 892215 (2022), which also bounds anomalous forces from supercurrents at `~5×10^-9 N/A`). The knowledgebase treats this correctly; this review adds only that the closure is complete enough that *no* gravitational reading of Tate should appear in any forward plan except as the definition of `η_g ≠ 1` falsification targets.

### 5.3 Hoang 2020: downgrade from "important but not secure" to "unaudited secondary quotation"

New facts established in this review and reconciliation: the paper is real (Mater. Lett. 262, 127176; nine authors; Hanoi University of Science and Technology; corresponding author Binh Xuan Cao), and a **corrigendum exists** (Mater. Lett. 281, 128597, December 2020) whose content could not be retrieved. The accessible original article page says the experiment used a spherical-shell method, reports an error analysis, and refers to supplementary apparatus material. That corrects the first review's claim that no supplement or apparatus description exists. The complete package has still not been obtained or audited by this project, no raw-data repository was located, and no independent replication was found. Consequently every quoted precision result remains provisional. The "absence of photographs" remark is not in Tajmar 2022, and "six type-I superconductors" cannot be correct as written because niobium is type II. Citation counts, venue, and group publication history are not used as technical rejection criteria.

Consequence: the difference between 92 ppm and 18 ppm should **not** currently be used as evidence of material/geometry dependence (the knowledgebase's §1.5 inference "it argues against a universal extra rest mass" rests on an unaudited number). The recommended handling is in the ranked moves: acquire the full text + corrigendum before this datum informs anything.

### 5.4 The entrainment channel is an underexplored search gap

Two targeted sweeps (rotating-superconductor literature; multicomponent-condensate literature) did not locate a treatment that combines an elastic lattice, condensate–lattice entrainment, and the Tate London coefficient in the proposed response-matrix form. This is a useful gap, but it is not proof that nothing exists: multicomponent rotational-response theory (for example Babaev–Ashcroft) and rotating-lattice/electrochemical treatments are adjacent. The searched Andreev–Bashkin literature still describes an established hydrodynamic effect with detection proposals; no direct laboratory measurement was located through the search date. A formal novelty review is required before claiming an unclaimed result.

### 5.5 A missing constraint the project should adopt: angular-momentum bookkeeping

Gyromagnetic experiments on superconductors measure mechanical angular momentum exchanged when magnetization changes; London-moment experiments measure the inverse. Kikoin–Gubar (J. Phys. USSR 3, 333 (1940)) and Pry, Lathrop, Houston (Phys. Rev. 86, 905 (1952)) report consistency with the free-electron relation, while Doll (Z. Phys. 153, 207 (1958)) and Hildebrandt (PRL 12, 190 (1964)) provide inverse-effect checks. In Li's picture the proposed flux-linked ionic current carries much more angular momentum per canceled charge current than the condensate. This makes the historical data a promising independent exclusion route. However, the `10^-2` accuracy and resulting six-order bound were not re-derived here from the original apparatus values, geometry, and uncertainties. Record the constraint as **provisional** until that derivation is completed; do not yet use it as a binding numerical kill.

### 5.6 `μ_g` and the modern gravity-side literature

The knowledgebase's position — retain the permeability analogy only as a prompt; replace the scalar with a derived kernel — is confirmed against the current literature. ## 6. The strongest case that the present lead is wrong or unproductive

1. **The anchor anomaly is one number from one apparatus, 36 years old.** Tate has not been repeated at comparable precision in the located literature. Its only claimed corroboration, Hoang 2020, cannot yet be admitted because the project has not audited the complete apparatus, uncertainty, raw-data, and corrigendum package. Penetration-depth extrapolation, trapped flux in type-II niobium, and geometry remain plausible systematic categories requiring a source-level reanalysis or a modern repeat.
2. **The theory around the anomaly is protective, not permissive.** Gauge structure, Galilean invariance, band-renormalization cancellation, and thermodynamic exactness pin the bulk London coefficient to the bare mass at the `10^-10` level. The response-matrix calculation, done honestly, will most likely prove a protection theorem — i.e., the project's flagship calculation is well positioned to *destroy* its own anchor, leaving the thread with no data at all.
3. **The neutral-mode algebra is not the obstruction; implementation and coupling are.** Opposite-charge mass currents add under charge cancellation, so mass asymmetry is unnecessary. The real walls are producing a large controllable circulating or angular mass current in a usable material, maintaining coherence and stability, accounting for its complete stress-energy, and supplying a source-to-gravity law beyond ordinary GR. Exciton and spin-counterflow modes must not be conflated: the former can carry neutral center-of-mass mass flow; the latter can cancel mass while retaining spin.
4. **The gravity side is uniformly negative.** Every enhanced-coupling claim in this domain has been refuted, retracted, or nulled — Li–Torr, Podkletnov, Tajmar–de Matos, gravito-Meissner, gravitational-Casimir enhancement — while derived-response theory (Kubo, EFT) finds ordinary stress-energy every time. There is no anomaly in any gravitational observable, anywhere, to explain.
5. **The scale gap to the capability model is not a gap but a chasm.** Even granting a resolved matter-sector anomaly at the full 92 ppm, the ordinary gravitational consequence of the implied stress-energy is ~20+ orders below anything in the capability stack, and the project has no candidate law for `η_g > 1`. A research program whose success case still sits 20 orders from its destination, with its only anomaly likely systematic, arguably should redirect its theory-selection effort toward a different P3 family (e.g., invariant worldtube conditions and source-side requirements) rather than deeper into superconductors.

## 7. The strongest technically responsible case for continuing

1. **The calculation is decisive within the matter sector and useful either way.** Targeted sweeps did not locate the exact rotating-superconductor response matrix proposed here. Whichever way it lands — protection theorem or loophole — it would quantify what the Tate observable can and cannot contain. Publication novelty must be established separately, and the result would not by itself solve the source-to-gravity problem.
2. **Tate is not a generic residual; it is a protected-observable residual.** The same rigidity that makes a conventional bulk explanation unlikely makes the anomaly, if real, extraordinarily informative: essentially only a second phase-rigid component or new coupling can move the bulk number. Nothing else in the project's evidence base has this property of "small, clean, and theoretically impossible-or-revolutionary." It is the correct place to spend adversarial effort.
3. **The reformulated principle is real physics with live neighbors.** Neutral center-of-mass flow is demonstrated in exciton systems, two-condensate rotational anomalies exist in theory (Babaev–Ashcroft), spin counterflow has a distinct current algebra, chiral-phonon angular momentum is mechanically measured, and entrainment detection proposals are maturing. The project is not inventing a category; it must now ask which realizations supply large controllable stress-energy and which are only analogies.
4. **The discipline generalizes.** The factorization `X_gA = G_gT · χ_TA`, the protection-theorem method, and the constraint ledger are exactly the machinery any future mechanism family (P3 alternatives) will need. Superconductors are the training ground where the method meets ppm data; abandoning the thread now would discard the project's best-developed analytical asset at the moment it becomes quantitative.
5. **The premise does not require this thread to be the mechanism.** The thread remains a useful discovery-mode instrument where "electrically quiet, mechanically active" can be defined, computed, and tested, but the phase-gated universal-field downselection now holds rank 1 because it addresses the missing interaction law.

## 8. Judgment

**Continue the thread as a matter-sector baseline and side project, not at rank 1.** The rejection case is strong against belief in a superconductor–gravity mechanism; it is weak against the calculation, which remains well posed and informative. The phase-gated universal-field downselection is the authoritative next major milestone because it tests the missing interaction and scale bridge directly.

Specifically: (i) run `00_Knowledgebase/NEXT-AGENT-PHASE-GATED-GRAVITY-PROMPT.md` next; (ii) include a compact conventional matter-gate baseline that explains what a London protection theorem would and would not establish; (iii) schedule the full §11 response-matrix derivation as a companion report if superconductivity survives as a leading gate or if a dedicated Tate decision is needed; (iv) keep gravitational terms out of the standalone matter-sector calculation; and (v) record the scale gap so a London anomaly cannot silently inherit craft-level expectations.
