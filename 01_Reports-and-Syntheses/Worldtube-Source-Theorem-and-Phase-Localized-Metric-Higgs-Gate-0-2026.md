## Executive verdict

The weakest useful worldtube is easy to specify and fantastically hard to source.

For terrestrial hovering, fixed interior worldlines must be nearly geodesic in the physical metric while the worldtube remains stationary relative to Earth. In a static chart this requires an approximately constant interior lapse, not merely a force on one material. For a \(10\) m cabin at \(1g\), the lapse change that must be removed is only

\[
\frac{g_0L}{c^2}=1.091\times10^{-15}.
\]

Earth's natural tide across the cabin is already only about \(3.1\times10^{-5}\ {\rm m\,s^{-2}}\), well inside a \(1\%\) requirement. The problem is therefore not a large metric amplitude. It is terminating a uniform metric gradient at a bounded shell while satisfying the Einstein constraints, conservation, and exterior matching.

An exact diagnostic metric makes the source cost explicit. Take spatially flat slices, zero shift, an inertial interior lapse, and the locally Rindler/Earth lapse outside. The Einstein tensor then requires **zero local energy density but enormous anisotropic shell pressure**:

\[
p_{\rm shell}\sim\frac{c^2g_0}{8\pi Gd}
=5.25\times10^{27}\ {\rm Pa}
\quad(d=0.10\ {\rm m}).
\]

The integrated stress is \(5.25\times10^{26}\ {\rm N,m^{-1}}\), independent of wall thickness at scaling level. A conventional Newtonian-gauge realization replaces this stress-only source with paired positive and negative effective mass. A quiet-exterior two-layer shell of radii 5.0 and 5.1 m requires surface-density amplitudes about \(\pm6\times10^{11}\ {\rm kg,m^{-2}}\) and a total absolute positive-plus-negative mass inventory about \(1.9\times10^{14}\) kg. Smoothing the layers over 0.1 m gives peak effective densities around \(3.4\times10^{13}\ {\rm kg,m^{-3}}\).

These are two representations of the same obstruction: a bounded field-gradient discontinuity needs an extraordinary source tensor even when the desired metric perturbation is tiny.

There is also a global motion constraint. A compact, conserved, universally coupled body follows the background geodesic at monopole order. Internal phase changes can produce only curvature/multipole forces unless they alter passive gravitational charge, communicate stress to an external source, emit momentum, or use exotic stress-energy. Near Earth, the natural finite-size parameter is \(R/R_E\simeq7.8\times10^{-7}\), far below the order-one change required for hover. Thus “field cancellation in the cavity” and “the whole device levitates” are separate claims.

A phase-localized dRGT-like metric mass has one narrow success. At the linear propagation level, a 0.10 m layer with in-medium graviton Compton wavenumber

The material window is not. No known order parameter supplies the required four reference/Stückelberg fields or switches a Planck-normalized spin-2 mass term. The phase-localized metric-Higgs concept is therefore not promoted as a craft mechanism. ## 2. The least-geometry hover metric

Choose a smooth radial switch \(f(r)\) with

\[
f=0\quad(r\le R),
\qquad
f=1\quad(r\ge R+d).
\]

Define

\[
\boxed{N(\mathbf x)=1+\frac{g_0z}{c^2}f(r),
\quad \beta^i=0,
\quad \gamma_{ij}=\delta_{ij}.}
\]

Inside, \(N=1\), so the fixed congruence is geodesic and flat. Outside, the leading metric is the local Rindler form of the Earth-supported frame. The shell contains all additional curvature.

This is not asserted to be the unique or globally optimal metric. It is the weakest transparent member of the target class: no shift, no interior spatial curvature, no exterior perturbation beyond the background.

### 2.1 Exact effective stress tensor

For

\[
ds^2=-N^2c^2dt^2+\delta_{ij}dx^idx^j,
\]

the nonzero Ricci components are

\[
R_{00}=N\nabla^2N,
\qquad
R_{ij}=-\frac1N\partial_i\partial_jN,
\]

and

\[
R=-\frac2N\nabla^2N.
\]

The Einstein tensor is

\[
G_{00}=0,
\]

\[
\boxed{G_{ij}=\frac1N
\left(\delta_{ij}\nabla^2N-\partial_i\partial_jN\right).}
\]

Therefore the required Eulerian energy density is exactly zero,

\[
\rho_E=T_{\mu\nu}n^\mu n^\nu=0,
\]

while the spatial stress is

\[
\boxed{T^{\rm req}_{ij}=\frac{c^4}{8\pi G N}
\left(\delta_{ij}\nabla^2N-\partial_i\partial_jN\right).}
\]

This is the complete stress tensor for this target metric. The price of keeping the spatial geometry exactly flat is a stress-only source.

For a locally planar boundary, \(N=N(z)\),

\[
T_{xx}=T_{yy}=\frac{c^4}{8\pi G}\frac{N''}{N},
\qquad
T_{zz}=0.
\]

Since \(N'\) changes by about \(g_0/c^2\) across \(d\),

\[
|p_\perp|\sim\frac{c^2g_0}{8\pi Gd}
=5.25\times10^{27}\ {\rm Pa}.
\]

The integrated transverse stress is

\[
\int p_\perp dz\sim\frac{c^2g_0}{8\pi G}
=5.25\times10^{26}\ {\rm N,m^{-1}}.
\]

Making the wall thinner increases local stress while leaving this integrated scale unchanged.

Because \(\rho_E=0\) but \(T_{ij}\ne0\), the dominant energy condition fails wherever the shell is active. In a closed \(l=1\)-type shell, the angular stress changes sign, so null/weak energy violations occur in parts of the wall as well. This is the exact missing source property for the flat-slice design.

## 5. Worldtube source theorem: why cavity cancellation is not whole-craft hover

The small-body theorems of Geroch–Jang and Ehlers–Geroch show that a sufficiently compact, conserved stress-energy satisfying appropriate positivity conditions follows a background geodesic. Multipolar equations make the correction explicit: spin couples to curvature and the quadrupole to curvature gradients, while the monopole follows geodesic motion.

For a compact craft of size \(R\) in Earth's field, the natural finite-size suppression is

\[
\epsilon_{\rm tide}\sim\frac{R}{R_E}
=7.84\times10^{-7}.
\]

An order-one hover force cannot be obtained by ordinary internal rearrangement of a universally coupled body in the locally uniform part of the field.

**Worldtube source theorem, engineering form.** A bounded low-tide metric cavity does not by itself establish controlled motion of the complete source–shell–payload system. To change the center-of-mass trajectory by \(O(g_0)\), at least one of the following must be present and explicit:

1. an external stress/field connection to Earth or another source;
2. emitted radiation or particles carrying momentum;
3. nonminimal coupling that changes passive gravitational response;
4. negative/exotic monopole or pressure contributions large enough to evade the positive-energy small-body premise;
5. a non-asymptotically-flat/global spacetime boundary that acts as the reservoir.

This is the precise role of the “momentum sink.” Static hover need not consume rocket-like power, but the global support interaction must exist. A magnetic superconductor levitates because its currents interact with an external magnetic source and field gradient; the magnet/Earth receives the opposite force. An exactly exterior-quiet compact gravity shell cannot simultaneously claim an ordinary Earth reaction without showing where the connecting stress or altered gravitational charge resides.

If Earth is the reservoir, its recoil per kilogram of hovering craft is

\[
\frac{a_E}{M_c}=\frac{g_0}{M_E}
=1.64\times10^{-24}\
\frac{{\rm m,s^{-2}}}{{\rm kg}}.
\]

The recoil is tiny but conceptually compulsory.

## 9. Decisive experiment

The next laboratory experiment should test **phase-dependent transmission of an externally generated gravitational field**, not thrust.

### Geometry

- A dense source mass is sinusoidally translated or rotated outside a nested cryogenic shell.
- Two matched differential detectors sit inside: one atom-interferometric or levitated sensor and one independent torsion/optomechanical channel.
- The inner shell is switched between normal and ordered states without moving the source or detectors.
- A matched non-superconducting cryogenic shell repeats the same thermal cycle.

For a \(1000\) kg source at 0.50 m, the direct acceleration scale is

\[
a_s=\frac{GM_s}{r^2}=2.67\times10^{-7}\ {\rm m,s^{-2}}.
\]

A \(1\%\) transmission change is \(2.7\times10^{-9}\ {\rm m,s^{-2}}\). The source modulation should move the signal away from thermal-cycle frequencies; the phase state should be modulated more slowly to create a sideband/difference-of-differences observable.

### Controls

- superconducting/normal reversal in the same shell;
- identical temperature waveform that does not cross \(T_c\);
- magnetic source-off runs and field mapping at every detector;
- electrostatic guard electrodes and charge monitoring;
- vacuum, accelerometers, microphones, seismometers, and laser metrology;
- reversed source-mass phase and dummy-mass runs;
- composition-paired detector bodies;
- external reference gravimeters outside the shell.

### Predictions

- Standard GR plus conventional superconductivity: no state-dependent transmission change beyond the known change in ordinary stress-energy divided by \(c^2\).
- Literal metric-mass shell with \(m_gd\gtrsim4.6\): order-one-to-99% attenuation, with a spatial transfer function fixed by the shell geometry.
- Generic phase-correlated artifact: detector-specific, magnetic/thermal, or geometry-independent signal failing the source-distance law.

### Falsification rule

Any specified metric-Higgs benchmark is excluded if the state-dependent attenuation is below the predicted transfer function after the shell's measured phase fraction and thickness are included. A null does not exclude every possible unknown phase portal; it directly closes the tested penetration-depth region.

## 10. Strongest rejection and continuation cases

### Strongest rejection case

The target metric itself forces the issue. A bounded \(1g\) gradient switch requires either \(10^{27}\) Pa stress-only matter or paired positive/negative effective mass at \(10^{14}\) kg scale for the reference geometry. A closed ordinary body cannot turn internal phase switching into monopole hover. The metric-Higgs action can create a formal penetration length but does not identify the material operator, finite-shell solution, or external momentum exchange. Treating \(m_g(s)\) as a free susceptibility would simply hide all missing physics in one coefficient.

### Strongest continuation case

The needed metric amplitude is extremely small, and a healthy scalar-controlled massive-spin-2 theory is not ruled out as a matter of formal field theory. The penetration mass is only micro-eV for a decimeter wall, and the homogeneous-phase strong-coupling scale is high. If a new spacetime-order sector exists whose phase controls a Planck-normalized potential and couples to an external field reservoir, it could act as a transistor: small switching work could redirect a much larger external field stress. The correct discovery target is therefore a reproducible phase-dependent **external transfer function**, not an unexplained self-force.

## Boundaries and non-claims

- The lapse-shell metric is a diagnostic inverse design, not a claim that a material realizes it.
- “Zero energy density” in that ansatz does not mean “free”; its stress is enormous and nonordinary.
- The two-surface calculation is a weak-field source representation, not a stable negative-mass construction.
- The dynamic shift energy is specific to the stated Alcubierre profile; it is not a universal numerical lower bound for every modified-gravity metric.
- Formal ghost freedom of a mass-varying dRGT action does not establish stability, causality, or radiative naturalness of a material interface.
- A phase-dependent transmission signal would be a new field phenomenon, not yet propulsion.
- Static hover can have zero steady mechanical power while still requiring an external support interaction and switching/formation energy.
