# Ning Li Superconductivity–Gravitomagnetism Program

Read these papers as one derivational chain:

1. `Gravitomagnetic field on SCs.pdf` — Li and Torr (1991), weak-field GEM plus London superconductivity; introduces the material-response question.
2. `Gravitational effects on magnetic attenuation SCs PhysRevB.46.5489.pdf` — Li and Torr (1992), proposes quantized lattice-ion angular momentum and a bound/free current model.
3. `cupdf.com_torr-and-li-1993-gravitoelectric-electric-coupling.pdf` — Torr and Li (1993), turns that model into the claimed large `μ_g` and an induced gravitoelectric response.
4. `Static test Gravitational Force YBCO.pdf` — Li et al. (1997), direct static YBCO test; null below 2 parts in `10^8` of local `g` for its apparatus.
